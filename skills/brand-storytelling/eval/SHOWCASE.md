# Showcase: Brand Storytelling

> Demonstrates the value of the `brand-storytelling` skill pack by comparing
> skill-guided output vs. a vanilla LLM response to the same prompt.

## Test Prompt

> Use `brand-storytelling`. Company: we're building a SOC2 automation tool. Audience: seed investors. Channels: pitch + podcast. Desired perception: credible, technical, direct, relentless. Raw material: I spent 18 months leading audits; the turning point was watching a team miss a launch because evidence was scattered. Proof: 3 design partners, 40% less audit prep time. Output: Brand Storytelling Pack.

## Key Differences

1. **Narrative focus vs. marketing breadth.** The skill output builds everything around one singular "five-second moment" and maps the narrative arc around it (context, tension, moment, new belief, action, proof, invitation). The baseline provides a broader marketing toolkit (taglines, competitive positioning, content templates) but distributes the narrative across multiple stories without the same focal point.

2. **Sayable scripts.** The skill output includes a 2-minute pitch script and a 30-second elevator version written as natural speech the founder can rehearse and deliver. The baseline provides a slide-by-slide pitch backbone and a podcast arc template but does not include word-for-word scripts the founder can practice.

3. **Proof integrity tracking.** The skill output maintains a proof bank with explicit verified/to-validate status per claim and a say/do alignment table that flags where the story makes promises the company must deliver on. The baseline deploys proof points effectively but does not track their validation status or flag alignment risks.

4. **Build-in-public plan.** The skill output includes a structured 3-pillar, 3x/week content cadence with guardrails (no customer names, no over-claims, founder review) and success signals. The baseline provides LinkedIn post templates and a 90-day rollout calendar, which is comparable in scope but less explicit about content guardrails.

5. **Q&A preparation.** The skill output includes a 10-question Q&A bank covering likely investor and podcast questions, each with a pithy 1-sentence answer, supporting evidence, and an optional longer follow-up. The baseline includes a podcast prep sheet with likely questions and response frames but without the structured short/long answer format.

## Verdict

The baseline is a more complete marketing toolkit, particularly strong on competitive positioning, voice guidelines, and content derivatives. The skill output is a more focused storytelling weapon, optimized for a founder who needs to walk into a pitch room or podcast studio with a rehearsed, proof-backed narrative and crisp answers. For a seed-stage founder, the skill output's scripts and Q&A bank are immediately higher-leverage.
