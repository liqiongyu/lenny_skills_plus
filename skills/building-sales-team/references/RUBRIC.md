# Rubric — Building Sales Team (Sales Team Build Pack)

Score each category **0–2**. Target: **≥ 10/14** with no category at **0**.

## 1) Readiness gate clarity
**0 — Absent:** No hire/wait decision; no metrics or milestones referenced; the plan jumps straight to hiring without addressing whether the motion is repeatable.
**1 — Partial:** A hire/wait decision exists with some supporting metrics or assumptions, but milestones are vague (e.g., "when pipeline is healthy") or the decision relies on gut feel rather than data.
**2 — Strong:** Decision is tied to specific, measurable evidence (e.g., win rate over N at-bats, pipeline coverage ratio). Milestones for re-evaluation are defined. If assumptions are made, they are labeled and a validation plan exists.

## 2) Sales motion spec (teachability)
**0 — Absent:** No written motion spec; guidance amounts to "sell the product." Stage definitions, pricing guardrails, and responsibilities are missing.
**1 — Partial:** A motion spec exists but has meaningful gaps — e.g., stages are listed without exit criteria, demo/pilot rules are missing, or founder-vs-rep responsibilities are unclear.
**2 — Strong:** Motion is fully documented with stage exit criteria, demo/pilot rules, pricing guardrails, common objections, and explicit founder-vs-rep handoff boundaries. A new rep could run 10 deals using this spec.

## 3) Team design + sequencing
**0 — Absent:** Org chart dropped in without rationale; roles are specialized prematurely for the stage; no hiring sequence or timing.
**1 — Partial:** Reasonable role selection with some rationale, but sequencing is under-explained or comparability (hire-two, A/B) is not addressed.
**2 — Strong:** Topology matches the selling motion (PLG vs outbound vs enterprise). Hiring sequence is justified with timing and dependencies. Comparability baseline (two reps or structured comparison) is planned.

## 4) Role scorecards (signals + tests)
**0 — Absent:** Generic traits ("self-starter," "hunter mentality") with no testable signals or evaluation criteria.
**1 — Partial:** Some concrete signals and must-haves exist, but not all map to interview tests. Technical/product depth bar is mentioned but not operationalized.
**2 — Strong:** Each role has specific outcomes, must-have signals, and red flags. Every signal maps to at least one interview exercise or reference-check question. Product/technical depth bar is explicit where applicable.

## 5) Interview loop quality
**0 — Absent:** Unstructured interviews; no practical exercises; no scoring rubric; no debrief protocol.
**1 — Partial:** Interview stages and some exercises exist, but scoring is inconsistent (e.g., no numeric rubric) or the debrief is informal.
**2 — Strong:** Structured stages with practical tests (mock discovery, objection handling, product deep-dive). Numeric scoring per signal. Evidence-based debrief protocol with explicit hire/no-hire criteria.

## 6) Onboarding + ramp + cadence
**0 — Absent:** "Shadow someone and figure it out." No ramp timeline, no metrics, no coaching cadence.
**1 — Partial:** A 30/60/90 plan exists with some milestones and basic cadence, but metrics are incomplete or the plan does not distinguish process failure from rep failure.
**2 — Strong:** Ramp targets are measurable at each phase (30/60/90). Coaching cadence is scheduled (weekly pipeline review, call coaching). A learning loop exists to diagnose process vs person (rep-to-rep comparison, cohort analysis).

## 7) Execution readiness (risks + next steps)
**0 — Absent:** No risks, open questions, or next steps section.
**1 — Partial:** Risks and next steps are listed but generic (e.g., "hiring is hard"). No owners or timelines assigned.
**2 — Strong:** Risks are specific and mitigated (e.g., "if first rep misses ramp targets by day 60, trigger X"). Open questions have owners and deadlines. Next steps are executable this week.

## Decision rule
- **Ship** if total score is **≥ 10/14** and no dimension scores **0**.
- **Revise** if any dimension scores **0** or total is below **10**.
