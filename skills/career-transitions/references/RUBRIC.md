# Rubric (Career Transitions) — Score 0/1/2

0 = missing or unusable | 1 = present but weak | 2 = clear, specific, and reusable

Suggested passing bar: **>= 7/10**

## 1) Clarity (0–2)
- **0:** Goals are vague; no constraints, no timeline, no progress metric. A reader cannot tell what “success” means.
- **1:** Some constraints and a progress metric exist, but the decision rule is unclear or contradictory; assumptions are not labeled.
- **2:** Crisp progress metric, explicit constraints with dates, clear decision rule (“I’ll choose path X if evidence Y by date Z”); trade-offs are explicit and the user can explain the plan in 60 seconds.

## 2) Options quality (0–2)
- **0:** Random list of roles with no archetypes and no fit signals; options are indistinguishable.
- **1:** 2-4 archetypes exist but overlap significantly; fit signals and no-go signals are vague or missing for at least one archetype.
- **2:** 2-4 distinct archetypes, each with specific fit signals, no-go signals, and a clear “why this could work” rationale tailored to the user’s progress metric.

## 3) Evidence + de-risking (0–2)
- **0:** No experiments; transition plan is based on opinions and assumptions with no plan to validate them.
- **1:** Experiments exist but are not mapped to the biggest unknowns, or they lack pass/fail signals and time bounds.
- **2:** Experiments are sequenced, timeboxed (specific weeks), mapped to the top unknowns, include pass/fail criteria, and learnings explicitly update the scorecard.

## 4) Pipeline realism (0–2)
- **0:** “Network more” with no specific people, no cadence, and no outreach scripts.
- **1:** List of people exists but lacks a weekly cadence, tracking system, or ready-to-send scripts; fewer than 30% have warm paths.
- **2:** Weekly cadence + tracking system + ready-to-send scripts; next 7 days are scheduled; strategy includes multiple channels (warm intros, communities, content) and reciprocity/goodwill.

## 5) Execution fit (0–2)
- **0:** Plan ignores constraints and runway; actions are impossible given stated time/money/energy limits.
- **1:** Plan is plausible but too broad — tries to pursue all options simultaneously without prioritization or a “stop doing” list.
- **2:** Plan fits time/runway, focuses on highest-leverage moves first, includes a “stop doing” list, protects energy, and builds momentum with small wins in week 1.

## Overall score guidance
- **0–3:** Not ready; redo intake and simplify options.
- **4–6:** Usable draft; tighten decision rule, pipeline cadence, and experiments.
- **7–10:** Strong; execute and iterate weekly.
