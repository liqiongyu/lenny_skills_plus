# Rubric — Coaching PMs (0–2 per dimension)

Score the PM Coaching Pack before finalizing. Total max = 12.

## 1) Definition clarity (in-context "good PM")
- **0:** Only vague traits ("be more strategic") with no observable behaviors; no connection to role scope or context.
- **1:** Some behaviors listed but missing scope/decision rights context or Bloom depth; expectations are partially generic.
- **2:** Clear observable behaviors tied to role scope + artifacts; context-specific competency model with Bloom depth ladder.

## 2) Evidence-based assessment
- **0:** Assessment is opinion-based; no concrete evidence or work examples cited.
- **1:** Some examples referenced but gaps are not clearly supported by evidence; skill gaps vs system constraints are not separated.
- **2:** Strong evidence mapped to competencies with specific artifact references; uncertainty is labeled; skill gaps are distinguished from environmental constraints.

## 3) Growth bet selection
- **0:** No prioritization; 4+ bets scattered across unrelated areas; no rationale for selection.
- **1:** 2-4 bets with partial rationale; success signals or timeframe are missing for some bets.
- **2:** 1-3 high-leverage bets with clear success signals, timeframe, and rationale tied to business priorities and PM aspirations.

## 4) Development plan specificity
- **0:** Generic advice ("improve strategic thinking"); no reps, artifacts, or measurement signals.
- **1:** Some actions defined but missing weekly reps, artifact requirements, or measurement; coach commitments are absent.
- **2:** Weekly reps + stretch assignment(s) + required artifacts + measurable signals + explicit coach commitments + timeline.

## 5) Coaching cadence & toolkit
- **0:** No cadence or toolkit; coaching is ad-hoc with no structure.
- **1:** Cadence exists but coaching prompts, artifact review points, or directive-mode exceptions are incomplete.
- **2:** Sustainable cadence + coaching prompts that develop independent reasoning + artifact review points + explicit "when to be directive" exceptions.

## 6) Follow-up & adaptation
- **0:** No follow-up mechanism; no way to track progress or adjust the plan.
- **1:** Tracker exists but review cadence is unclear or there is no explicit mechanism to adjust bets when constraints change.
- **2:** Tracker + review checkpoints + explicit adjust/iterate loop with criteria for when to change bets or escalate.

## Interpreting scores
- **10–12:** Ship as-is.
- **7–9:** Ship with explicit assumptions + unknowns list.
- **< 7:** Do another intake round (max 5 questions) before finalizing.
