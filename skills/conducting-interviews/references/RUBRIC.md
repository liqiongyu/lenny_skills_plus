# Rubric (1–5)

Score the Interview Execution Pack. A strong pack is typically **≥ 22/30** with no category below 3.

## 1) Stage goal clarity (1–5)
- **0 (missing):** No stage goal stated; purpose is completely absent.
- **1:** Unclear purpose; overlaps with other stages; no timeboxes.
- **2:** Purpose exists but is multi-sentence and vague; timeboxes are missing or unrealistic (e.g., 60 min of questions in a 30-min screen).
- **3:** Purpose is mostly clear but stage responsibilities are still fuzzy; timeboxes exist but lack buffer for candidate questions.
- **4:** Clear one-sentence goal with realistic timeboxes; minor gaps in stage boundary definition (e.g., unclear handoff to next interviewer).
- **5:** One-sentence goal, crisp boundaries, and realistic timeboxes (including candidate questions).

## 2) Criteria quality (1–5)
- **0 (missing):** No evaluation criteria defined.
- **1:** Criteria are missing or pure “traits/vibes” (e.g., “smart”, “culture fit” with no definition).
- **2:** Criteria exist but are generic (e.g., “communication skills”) without role-specific anchors or evidence hints.
- **3:** Criteria exist but anchors are vague or not observable; fewer than 4 criteria or more than 8.
- **4:** 4-8 criteria with definitions and most have strong/weak anchors; one or two anchors lack concrete evidence examples.
- **5:** 4–8 criteria with clear definitions, strong/weak anchors, and evidence hints.

## 3) Question design (1–5)
- **0 (missing):** No question map provided.
- **1:** Generic questions; double-barreled; mostly hypotheticals (e.g., “What would you do if...”).
- **2:** Questions exist but most are hypothetical or not mapped to criteria; probes are absent.
- **3:** Some behavioral questions but mapping/probes are inconsistent; some questions cover multiple criteria.
- **4:** Mostly behavioral; each maps to one criterion; probes exist but some lack depth (e.g., missing counterfactual or “what would you do differently” follow-ups).
- **5:** Behavioral-first, one question -> one signal, with probing ladders that force specifics (role, constraints, trade-offs, results, retrospective).

## 4) Evidence capture + scoring (1–5)
- **0 (missing):** No scorecard or notes template.
- **1:** No structured notes/scorecard; ratings are impressionistic (e.g., “thumbs up/down” with no evidence).
- **2:** Scorecard has ratings but no space for evidence capture; or notes template exists but is free-form without structure.
- **3:** Scorecard exists but evidence requirements are weak or inconsistent; summary may not align with individual criterion scores.
- **4:** Scorecard requires evidence per rating; summary exists but does not explicitly reconcile with individual scores.
- **5:** Notes + scorecard demand evidence per rating; summary is consistent with criterion scores; overall recommendation explicitly references the ratings.

## 5) Substance-over-polish hygiene (1–5)
- **0 (missing):** No mention of substance vs polish or bias in delivery assessment.
- **1:** Rewards confidence/structure; penalizes candidates for communication style; no awareness of the problem.
- **2:** Mentions the risk in passing but provides no concrete countermeasures or examples.
- **3:** Acknowledges the risk but lacks concrete countermeasures; may say “look for substance” without explaining how.
- **4:** Includes specificity probes for polished communicators OR structural support for less polished candidates, but not both.
- **5:** Explicit guardrails to reduce false positives/negatives (specificity tests + question clarification for both ends of the spectrum).

## 6) Fairness + candidate experience (1–5)
- **0 (missing):** No consideration of candidate experience or fairness.
- **1:** Process feels adversarial; risks inappropriate topics; unclear next steps for the candidate.
- **2:** Basic structure exists but no explicit bias-reduction measures; close is generic (“we'll be in touch”).
- **3:** Candidate experience is acceptable but inconsistently handled; some bias-reduction steps exist.
- **4:** Clear expectations and respectful close; bias-reduction measures exist but are not comprehensive (e.g., no note-taking discipline or calibration step).
- **5:** Clear expectations, respectful close, and fairness hygiene (notes discipline, bias reduction, escalation to HR/legal when needed).
