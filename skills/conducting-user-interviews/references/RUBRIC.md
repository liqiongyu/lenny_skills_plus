# Rubric (Conducting User Interviews)

Score each dimension **1–5** (1 = poor, 3 = acceptable, 5 = excellent). Include notes and a short improvement plan for any score ≤ 3.

## 1) Decision clarity
- **1:** No decision; interviews are “general learning” with no stated outcome.
- **2:** A vague goal exists (“learn about users”) but no specific decision or deadline.
- **3:** Decision exists but is broad; success criteria are vague; unknowns are not listed.
- **4:** Decision and deadline are stated; unknowns are listed but success definition is soft.
- **5:** Clear decision + deadline + success definition; unknowns are explicit and prioritized.

## 2) Participant fit
- **1:** Participants are convenient but mismatched (e.g., internal colleagues, friends); no recency or behavioral criteria.
- **2:** Some criteria exist but are demographic-only (e.g., “enterprise users”) without behavioral/situational filters.
- **3:** Mostly fits; behavioral criteria are present but some interviews are low-signal; edge cases not planned.
- **4:** Strong behavior/situation criteria; recency prioritized; minor gaps in edge-case coverage.
- **5:** Strong fit with behavior/situation criteria; deliberate edge cases included; recency prioritized when relevant; “who NOT to interview” is explicit.

## 3) Guide quality (story-first)
- **1:** Mostly opinions/hypotheticals (“What do you think of…?”); leading questions; no probes.
- **2:** Some story questions but mixed with leading or double-barreled items; no time boxes.
- **3:** Mix of story + opinion; probes are inconsistent; time boxes exist but are loose.
- **4:** Majority story-first; neutral wording; probes exist for key topics; minor gaps.
- **5:** 70%+ story elicitation (“last time…”), neutral wording, clear probes and time boxes; consent/recording script included.

## 4) Execution and evidence capture
- **1:** Sparse notes; few or no verbatim quotes; no debrief after sessions.
- **2:** Notes exist but lack structure; quotes are paraphrased; debriefs are occasional.
- **3:** Adequate notes; some verbatim quotes; debriefs happen but are inconsistent or unstructured.
- **4:** Clean notes with verbatims; consistent debrief; observation vs interpretation mostly separated.
- **5:** Clean notes + verbatim quotes per session; consistent immediate debrief; clear separation of observation vs interpretation; “needs follow-up” items flagged.

## 5) Synthesis quality
- **1:** List of anecdotes with no clustering; contradictions ignored; no confidence levels.
- **2:** Some grouping attempted but themes are vague; evidence counts missing.
- **3:** Themes exist with partial evidence; confidence levels are unclear; contradictions mentioned but not explored.
- **4:** Themes tied to outcomes with evidence counts; segmentation considered; contradictions explored.
- **5:** Themes tied to outcomes; evidence strength and segmentation are explicit; contradictions resolved or flagged; insights translate into prioritized opportunities with confidence levels.

## 6) Actionability
- **1:** No recommendations or next steps; report ends at findings.
- **2:** Generic recommendations (“improve onboarding”); no owners or validation plan.
- **3:** Recommendations exist and are tied to themes but are broad; no validation steps.
- **4:** Specific recommendations with prioritization; some validation steps proposed.
- **5:** Recommendations are specific, scoped, and paired with validation steps and owners (where possible); customer panel / follow-up cadence defined.

## 7) Ethics and safety
- **1:** No consent or recording clarity; PII exposed; privacy risks ignored entirely.
- **2:** Consent mentioned but not scripted; recording policy unclear; data storage not addressed.
- **3:** Basic consent script included; recording policy stated; storage/retention details are unclear.
- **4:** Consent + recording are explicit; data minimization practiced; minor gaps in retention policy.
- **5:** Consent and minimization are explicit; risks and mitigations are documented; storage, retention, and deletion policies are clear; high-risk cases are gated.

## Suggested overall rating
- **Excellent:** avg ≥ 4.2, no dimension < 4
- **Good:** avg 3.5–4.1, no dimension < 3
- **Needs work:** avg < 3.5 or any dimension < 3
