# Rubric — Cross-functional Collaboration (1–5)

Score the Collaboration Pack before finalizing. Total score helps decide whether to proceed with assumptions or do another intake round.

## 1) Mission clarity + success metrics
1 = Vague mission; no metrics; no timeframe
2 = Mission exists but success metrics are missing or unmeasurable; timeframe is “ASAP”
3 = Mission stated; some metrics/constraints unclear
4 = Mission, metrics, and timeframe are all present; minor gaps in testability
5 = Mission + metrics + timeframe are explicit and testable; a cross-functional partner can restate them without help

## 2) Stakeholder completeness + incentives
1 = Missing key stakeholders; incentives unknown
2 = Major functions are listed but no incentives, concerns, or approvers are captured
3 = Stakeholders listed; incentives partially captured
4 = All executing and approving stakeholders identified; incentives captured for most; 1-2 gaps remain
5 = Full map; incentives/concerns clear; no surprise approvers likely; “missing seats” list is empty or explicitly addressed

## 3) Roles, expectations, and decision rights
1 = “Everyone owns it”; decision rights unclear
2 = Responsibilities assigned at function level but no escalation triggers or review cadence
3 = Responsibilities exist; escalation triggers vague
4 = Contract covers responsibilities, decision rights, and escalation triggers; review cadence is present but untested
5 = Contract is explicit; decision rights + triggers + review cadence are clear; each function can answer “What do I own?”

## 4) Shared artifacts and source of truth
1 = No artifact plan; doc sprawl likely
2 = Artifacts mentioned but no single source of truth designated; formats inconsistent
3 = Some artifacts; source of truth unclear
4 = Artifact set defined with a source of truth; no prototype/working slice to reduce ambiguity
5 = Minimal artifact set; clear source of truth; prototype/working slice reduces ambiguity

## 5) Operating cadence + decision velocity
1 = Meeting-heavy or no cadence; decisions re-litigated
2 = Cadence defined but decision log is missing; updates are status-only
3 = Cadence exists; decision log partially used
4 = Cadence is reasonable; decision log is seeded; some risk of status theater remains
5 = Cadence is sustainable; decisions are logged; velocity improves without status theater

## 6) Collaboration norms (conflict + credit)
1 = Conflict avoided or personal; credit hoarded
2 = Generic norms (“be respectful”) with no specific conflict or credit protocol
3 = Some norms; inconsistently applied
4 = Conflict protocol and credit plan exist; specificity is adequate but untested
5 = Conflict protocol is usable in a real disagreement; credit sharing is explicit and practiced in exec/customer updates

## Interpreting scores
- **26–30:** ship as-is
- **20–25:** ship with explicit assumptions + a short “unknowns” list
- **< 20:** do another intake round (max 5 questions) before finalizing
