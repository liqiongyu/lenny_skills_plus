# Rubric — Delegating Work (0–2 per dimension)

Score the Delegation Pack before finalizing. Total max = 12.

## 1) Outcome clarity
- **0:** No definition of done; outcome is vague ("make it better") or missing entirely.
- **1:** Outcome is stated but lacks measurable acceptance criteria or testable success signals.
- **2:** Outcome + acceptance criteria are explicit, testable, and the delegatee can say "done" unambiguously.

## 2) Context transfer
- **0:** Only tasks assigned; no "why", no prior decisions, no known pitfalls shared.
- **1:** Some context provided (e.g., background docs linked) but missing prior decisions, tradeoffs, or stakeholder landscape.
- **2:** Full context pack: the "why", prior decisions, known pitfalls, stakeholder map, and example outputs; owner can reason independently.

## 3) Decision rights + guardrails
- **0:** No autonomy level stated; unclear who decides what; no escalation triggers.
- **1:** Autonomy level mentioned but escalation triggers are vague ("if it feels risky") or review points are missing.
- **2:** Explicit autonomy level per decision area, specific escalation triggers (quantified or event-based), and scheduled review points.

## 4) Empowerment vs micromanagement
- **0:** Manager prescribes step-by-step instructions; owner is reduced to executor with no room for judgment.
- **1:** Mixed signals: some autonomy granted but manager overrides decisions or requests task-level status updates.
- **2:** Criteria-based leadership; manager reviews artifacts against quality bar and constraints, intervenes only at guardrail breaches.

## 5) Operating cadence
- **0:** No check-in cadence defined, or cadence is daily task-level status (micromanagement).
- **1:** Cadence exists but updates focus on activities/tasks rather than outcomes, risks, and decisions.
- **2:** Cadence is outcome/decision/risk oriented, sustainable frequency, and update format is defined.

## 6) Closure + learning
- **0:** No debrief planned; ownership snaps back to manager after delivery.
- **1:** Debrief exists but learnings are not captured as template/process improvements; ownership durability is unclear.
- **2:** Debrief captured with specific learnings; ownership is explicitly made durable; process improvements are documented.

## Interpreting scores
- **10–12:** Ship as-is.
- **7–9:** Ship with explicit assumptions + a short "unknowns" list.
- **< 7:** Do another intake round (max 5 questions) before finalizing.
