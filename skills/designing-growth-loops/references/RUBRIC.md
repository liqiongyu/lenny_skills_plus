# RUBRIC (Designing Growth Loops)

Score each item 0/1/2:
- 0 = missing / unusable
- 1 = present but incomplete
- 2 = clear, executable, reusable

Suggested passing bar: **>= 13/16**.

## 1) Decision usefulness (0–2)
- **0** — No decision framed; output is brainstorming or a generic list of growth ideas with no "what we'll do differently."
- **1** — A decision is named (e.g., "build a referral loop") but implications, trade-offs, or "what changes if it fails" are fuzzy.
- **2** — Clear decision + clear "what we'll do differently" based on outcomes; stakeholder can act on the pack without follow-up clarification.

## 2) Loop clarity — micro + macro (0–2)
- **0** — Tactics list with no feedback paths; no loop structure (input, action, output, re-entry) is defined.
- **1** — Some loops are defined but connections between micro and macro loops are weak; bottlenecks are not identified or cycle times are missing.
- **2** — Clear micro loops + coherent macro loop; bottlenecks and cycle times are explicit; someone unfamiliar with the product can explain "how we grow" in 60 seconds using the map.

## 3) Evidence grounding (0–2)
- **0** — No baseline metrics; no assumptions labeled; claims are unsupported opinions.
- **1** — Baseline exists but is incomplete (e.g., acquisition volume without conversion or retention); confidence levels on assumptions are unclear.
- **2** — Baseline + assumptions + confidence are explicit; every claim is tied to evidence or labeled as a hypothesis with a plan to validate.

## 4) Prioritization quality (0–2)
- **0** — No scorecard or ranking; "pick everything" or arbitrary selection with no rationale.
- **1** — Scorecard or ranking exists but selection rationale is thin; trade-offs between top candidates are not articulated.
- **2** — Scorecard drives a clear top 1-2 loop selection with trade-offs, rationale, and an explicit innovate-vs-optimize recommendation.

## 5) Unit economics + paid gating (0–2)
- **0** — Recommends paid acquisition loops without any LTV/CAC/payback reasoning; or paid loops are included with no economic viability check.
- **1** — Mentions unit economics but no clear go/no-go gate; prerequisites for paid viability are vague.
- **2** — Paid loop viability is explicitly gated with LTV/CAC/payback math; prerequisites are listed if not currently viable; non-paid loops have resource/effort estimates.

## 6) Measurement + instrumentation readiness (0–2)
- **0** — Metrics are vague ("track growth"); no instrumentation plan or event schema.
- **1** — KPIs are defined but data sources, event properties, or instrumentation gaps are unclear.
- **2** — Loop KPIs + leading indicators defined; required events/properties specified; instrumentation gaps identified with fixes; dashboards outlined.

## 7) Actionability (0–2)
- **0** — Vague recommendations ("go viral," "leverage network effects") with no concrete experiments or timeline.
- **1** — Some experiments exist but sequencing, dependencies, or owners are missing; next 2 weeks of work are not clearly unblocked.
- **2** — Prioritized experiment backlog + 30/60/90 plan; next 2 weeks are unblocked; experiments have win/lose/learn criteria.

## 8) Safety + ethics (0–2)
- **0** — Encourages manipulative tactics (spam invites, misleading sharing, dark patterns) or ignores platform policy risk.
- **1** — Mentions safety/ethics but lacks specific guardrails for platform compliance, spam thresholds, or user consent.
- **2** — Explicit policy/ethics constraints; platform-specific compliance checks; risks and mitigations are concrete; user consent and spam thresholds are addressed.

### Interpretation (suggested)
- **13–16:** Strong; execute and review cadence weekly.
- **9–12:** Good directional; tighten weak areas before big bets.
- **<9:** Rework inputs/baseline/loop definitions; too speculative.
