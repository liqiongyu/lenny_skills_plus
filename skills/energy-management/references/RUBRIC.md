# Rubric (Score 0/1/2) — Energy Management

0 = missing or unusable | 1 = present but weak | 2 = clear, specific, and reusable

Suggested passing bar: **>= 7/10**

## 1) Goal clarity + boundaries (0–2)
- **0:** No success definition; safety boundaries are absent; the user cannot tell what “better” means in 4-8 weeks.
- **1:** Goals are mostly clear and basic boundaries are acknowledged, but the success definition is qualitative only (“feel less tired”) with no observable signals; 2-week pilot scope is vague.
- **2:** Crisp 4-8 week success definition with 2-3 observable signals (e.g., “end 3+ days/week with energy above 6/10, complete 4 deep-work blocks/week”); 2-week pilot scope is explicit; safety boundaries are stated and integrated into step 1.

## 2) Drivers/drains diagnosis quality (0–2)
- **0:** Generic labels (“meetings drain me”) with no patterns, triggers, or controllability assessment; fewer than 3 concrete items.
- **1:** Some specific drivers/drains are identified (5+), but triggers are partial, controllability is not assessed, and top drains lack owners or concrete next actions.
- **2:** Clear drivers/drains map with triggers, controllability rating (can remove / can redesign / must tolerate), and specific levers; top 3 drains each have an owner, a lever, and a next action with a date.

## 3) Calendar + zone-of-genius redesign quality (0–2)
- **0:** No calendar changes proposed; output is limited to coping tactics (breathing exercises, walks) without structural redesign.
- **1:** Some calendar changes are proposed (e.g., “add a focus block”) but the zone-of-genius expansion plan is incomplete — fewer than 2 concrete deletions/redesigns, or the energy-aligned default week lacks buffers.
- **2:** Concrete deletions/redesigns of 3+ energy drains; zone-of-genius expansion plan with stop/delegate/automate/defer items; energy-aligned default week with time blocks matched to energy curve, buffers between high-load blocks, and meeting hygiene rules.

## 4) Delegation/offload effectiveness (0–2)
- **0:** “Delegate more” with no specific items, no decision rights, and no guardrails.
- **1:** Some offload items are named, but delegation briefs are incomplete — missing decision rights, escalation triggers, or review points for at least half the items.
- **2:** Clear stop/delegate/automate/defer plan where each delegated item has decision rights, escalation triggers, a review cadence, and a named delegate; at least 2 concrete energy taxes are removed or redesigned in the first 2 weeks.

## 5) Measurement + sustainability (0–2)
- **0:** No pilot, no measurement criteria, no recovery plan; changes are presented as permanent commitments with no iteration mechanism.
- **1:** Pilot exists and some measures are defined, but experiments lack decision rules (keep/modify/stop) or the recovery plan is generic (“take breaks”).
- **2:** 2-4 testable experiments each with a specific change, measurement signal, and decision rule after 2 weeks; recovery and transition routines are scheduled (not aspirational) and include a low-energy-day protocol.

## Interpreting the score
- **9–10:** Ready to execute immediately.
- **7–8:** Solid; tighten the weakest area.
- **< 7:** Rework diagnosis, calendar redesign, or measurement before shipping.
