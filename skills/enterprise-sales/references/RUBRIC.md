# Enterprise Sales — Scoring Rubric

Score each item **0/1/2**:
- 0 = missing / unusable
- 1 = present but incomplete
- 2 = clear, executable, reusable

Suggested passing bar: **>= 10/14**.

## 1) Deal clarity + enterprise fit (0-2)
- **0:** No deal snapshot. Account, use case, ACV, timeline, or stage are missing or entirely vague. Cannot determine whether this is actually an enterprise deal.
- **1:** Account and timeline exist, but some key fields are assumed without being labeled. Stage is identified but success definition or top stall risk is unclear.
- **2:** Clear deal snapshot with explicit account, use case, ACV range, timeline, success definition, and the #1 stall risk identified. Assumptions are labeled with a validation plan.

## 2) Buying committee map + champion plan (0-2)
- **0:** No committee map. Champion not identified. Stakeholders listed as a flat list without roles, goals, or concerns.
- **1:** Map exists with 3+ roles and a champion candidate is identified, but goals/risks/evidence needs are incomplete for some stakeholders. Champion next actions are vague.
- **2:** Complete map with 5-7 roles, each with goals, risks, required evidence, and next actions. A primary champion is named with a concrete enablement plan and timeline. Backup path exists if champion is lost.

## 3) Champion enablement kit quality (0-2)
- **0:** No enablement materials, or materials are generic product brochures the champion cannot forward as-is.
- **1:** Internal pitch memo and some stakeholder one-pagers exist, but they are not tailored to each stakeholder's concerns. Objection answers are generic.
- **2:** Forwardable internal pitch memo + tailored one-pagers for IT/security, procurement, legal, and economic buyer + crisp objection/FAQ answers. Champion can copy/paste and use immediately without editing.

## 4) Decision enablement — "no decision" prevention (0-2)
- **0:** No decision-enablement assets. Relies on persuasion or discounting. MAP is absent. "Do nothing" cost not addressed.
- **1:** MAP exists and decision criteria are partly defined, but "do nothing" cost is not quantified. MAP lacks buyer commitments or a decision meeting date.
- **2:** "Do nothing" cost is explicit and quantified. Decision guide with options and criteria is complete. MAP has dates, owners, buyer commitments, and a decision meeting date.

## 5) POC/pilot design as business case (0-2)
- **0:** POC is a feature test with no success criteria, timeline, or business case framing. Or POC section is missing entirely.
- **1:** Metrics and timeline exist, but ROI model is rough and decision criteria are partial. Pilot is not clearly framed as producing a business case.
- **2:** 30-day pilot produces a decision-ready business case with measurable success metrics, ROI model with assumptions, decision criteria, and a decision date. Paid pilot option is considered.

## 6) Procurement + security execution plan (0-2)
- **0:** Procurement and security treated as "waiting for them to respond." No tracker, no owners, no dates. Forms and questionnaires mentioned but not organized.
- **1:** Tracker exists with some owners and dates, but missing a complete packet index, comms cadence, or escalation path. Some tasks lack clear owners.
- **2:** Clear tracker with owners, dates, and blockers for every procurement/security task. Security packet index is complete. Pre-filled forms offered. Proactive comms cadence and escalation plan defined.

## 7) Close-to-implementation readiness + completeness (0-2)
- **0:** Pack stops at "close the deal" or signature. No implementation handoff, no kickoff plan. Risks/Open questions/Next steps missing.
- **1:** Some implementation planning exists and next steps are listed, but kickoff and first-value milestones are vague. Risks/Open questions present but thin.
- **2:** Signature plan (who signs, when) + kickoff schedule + first-value milestones are explicit. Complete Risks/Open questions/Next steps. All assumptions labeled with validation plan.
