# Rubric (score the Candidate Evaluation Decision Pack)

Score each category 0/1/2:
- 0 = missing / unusable
- 1 = present but incomplete
- 2 = clear, decision-ready, evidence-based

Suggested passing bar: **>= 12/16**

## 1) Decision framing (0–2)
- **0:** No role/level stated; no decision deadline; signals are not identified. The reader cannot tell what decision is being made or by when.
- **1:** Role and level are stated but success outcomes are vague or missing; decision deadline exists but signals included/excluded lack rationale.
- **2:** Role, level, success outcomes, and decision deadline are explicit. Signals included/excluded are stated with rationale. A reader can answer: “What are we deciding, by when, using what evidence?”

## 2) Criteria + bar clarity (0–2)
- **0:** No criteria defined, or criteria are pure traits (“smart”, “culture fit”) without observable definitions. No red flags identified.
- **1:** Criteria exist (3-8) but definitions are vague or not consistently observable; red flags are generic or missing for some criteria.
- **2:** 4-8 criteria with observable definitions and strong/weak anchors. Red flags are explicit, role-relevant, and distinguishable from “nice-to-have weaknesses.”

## 3) Evidence quality (0–2)
- **0:** No evidence cited; ratings are pure opinion or adjective-based (“great communicator”, “strong leader”).
- **1:** Some evidence exists (examples or quotes) but coverage is uneven — some criteria lack supporting evidence; confidence/limitations are not stated.
- **2:** Evidence is specific (examples, quotes, outcomes) for every criterion. Confidence level and limitations are stated. A skeptical reader can trace each rating to concrete data.

## 4) Work sample/trial quality (0–2)
- **0:** No work sample/trial designed; or task is a brainteaser with no job relevance. (Score 0 if work sample was not part of the evaluation plan and mark N/A in notes.)
- **1:** Task is somewhat job-relevant but scoping is off (too long, too vague, or unfairly advantaging certain backgrounds); rubric exists but anchors are unclear.
- **2:** Task is job-relevant, scoped (clear time/deliverable expectations), and fairly scored. Rubric has concrete anchors that map to evaluation criteria. Paid trial has IP/confidentiality boundaries stated.

## 5) Reference check quality (0–2)
- **0:** No references conducted; or notes are just “positive reference.” (Score 0 if references were not part of the plan and mark N/A in notes.)
- **1:** Reference notes exist but rely on generic questions or candidate-provided references only; summary lacks specific examples or rehire signal.
- **2:** Reference notes include specific examples, calibration for bias, and longitudinal context. Summary yields a clear rehire signal with supporting evidence. Back-channel or extended-working-relationship references are prioritized.

## 6) Synthesis coherence (0–2)
- **0:** No synthesis or recommendation; or recommendation contradicts the evidence presented (e.g., “hire” despite multiple red flags with no mitigation).
- **1:** Recommendation exists but does not address all red flags or disconfirming evidence; weighting of signals is implicit rather than explicit.
- **2:** Recommendation matches weighted evidence and explicitly addresses red flags. Disconfirming evidence is included and reconciled. Two readers would reach the same conclusion from the memo.

## 7) Risks + mitigations (0–2)
- **0:** No risks section; or risks are trivially generic (“candidate might not work out”).
- **1:** Risks are identified but are vague or lack actionable mitigations (e.g., “monitor closely” without specifying checkpoints or metrics).
- **2:** Risks are concrete, plausible, and role-specific. Mitigations are actionable with owners and timelines (onboarding plan, 30/60/90 checkpoints, coaching support, exit criteria).

## 8) Fairness + privacy (0–2)
- **0:** Process relies on “vibes” or unstructured impressions; output contains raw PII or sensitive information without redaction.
- **1:** Some bias-reduction steps exist (e.g., structured scoring) but are inconsistently applied; privacy handling is mentioned but not operationalized.
- **2:** Process reduces bias systematically (independent scoring before debrief, criteria locked before evaluation, diverse signal sources). Output respects privacy/PII constraints with explicit redaction or anonymization where needed.
