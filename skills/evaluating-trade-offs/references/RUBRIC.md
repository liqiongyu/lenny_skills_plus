# Rubric (score the Trade-off Evaluation Pack)

Score each category 0/1/2:
- 0 = missing / unusable
- 1 = present but incomplete
- 2 = clear, decision-ready, and internally consistent

Suggested passing bar: **>= 10/12**

## 1) Framing + options (0–2)
- **0**: No clear decision statement; options are vague or missing; no date or owner identified.
- **1**: Decision is stated but missing a date or owner; options exist but overlap or “do nothing” is omitted when relevant.
- **2**: Decision is crisp, dated, and has an owner; options are concrete and mutually distinguishable.

## 2) Criteria + horizon (0–2)
- **0**: No criteria defined; time horizon is absent; everything is “important.”
- **1**: Some criteria listed but fewer than 4 or more than 8 without weights; time horizon is vague (“soon”).
- **2**: 4–8 criteria (incl. guardrail) + explicit time horizon; “not optimizing for” is stated to prevent goalpost moving.

## 3) All-in cost + opportunity cost (0–2)
- **0**: Only direct dollar costs considered; no opportunity cost; hidden costs ignored.
- **1**: Costs cover multiple dimensions but miss hidden items (maintenance, coordination); opportunity cost mentioned but not per-option.
- **2**: Costs are complete (incl. hidden costs) and comparable; opportunity cost (displaced work) is explicit per option.

## 4) Impact ranges + assumptions (0–2)
- **0**: Single-point estimates with false precision; assumptions unstated.
- **1**: Ranges exist for some options; key assumptions are partially identified but not tied to the model.
- **2**: Ranges + confidence are used; no false precision; top 2-3 assumptions driving the decision are named and testable.

## 5) Thought experiments + validation plan (0–2)
- **0**: No pre-mortem; no failure mode analysis; no validation plan.
- **1**: Pre-mortem is generic (“it could fail”); validation plan exists but tests the wrong assumption or is too expensive.
- **2**: Pre-mortem produces specific failure modes; minimal validation plan targets the biggest assumption with the cheapest evidence.

## 6) Recommendation quality (0–2)
- **0**: No recommendation; or recommendation contradicts the analysis; no next steps.
- **1**: Recommendation exists but rationale is thin or trade-offs are unstated; review date is missing.
- **2**: Recommendation is explicit with rationale and trade-offs; includes **Risks / Open questions / Next steps** + a review date / triggers.
