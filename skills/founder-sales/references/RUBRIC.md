# Founder Sales — Scoring Rubric

Score each item **0/1/2**:
- 0 = missing / unusable
- 1 = present but incomplete
- 2 = clear, executable, reusable

Suggested passing bar: **>= 10/14**.

## 1) ICP wedge clarity (0-2)
- **0:** No ICP defined, or ICP is so broad it could be anyone ("companies that need software"). No exclusion criteria. Buyer vs user not distinguished.
- **1:** ICP names a segment and role, but exclusion criteria are vague or missing. Buyer/user distinction mentioned but not actionable. A target buyer could not self-identify in 10 seconds.
- **2:** Sharp wedge with specific triggers, buyer/user roles, explicit exclusions, and a "repels non-ICP" test. A target buyer can self-identify in 10 seconds.

## 2) Founder POV strength (0-2)
- **0:** Generic feature pitch. No founder credibility angle. Reads like a product datasheet, not a founder's insight.
- **1:** Some insight narrative and proof points are present, but the POV is partly generic or feature-led. Founder credibility is mentioned but not woven into the outreach story.
- **2:** Clear insight-led POV that leads with the problem (not features), includes credible founder angle, and has proof points or a concrete plan to generate them within the sprint.

## 3) Outreach kit quality (0-2)
- **0:** Spammy, high-volume, or generic messages. No personalization hooks. CTA is unclear or asks for too much too soon.
- **1:** Reasonable sequence with some personalization hooks and a CTA, but messages could be tighter. Warm intro request and cold sequence exist but are not clearly differentiated.
- **2:** Ethical, targeted outreach with warm + cold variants, specific personalization hypotheses to test, clear single-ask CTAs, and a follow-up cadence designed for learning (not volume).

## 4) Discovery quality — diagnostic-first (0-2)
- **0:** Demo-driven script or no discovery script at all. Questions are generic ("Tell me about your pain points"). No call notes template.
- **1:** Mostly diagnostic questions exist and a notes template is present, but impact quantification is weak and the script may still default to showing the product too early. Next step at call end is vague.
- **2:** Diagnostic script that surfaces pain, impact, and decision process without defaulting to a demo. Ends with a concrete next step. Includes verbatim-capture guidance for messaging iteration.

## 5) Decision enablement — reducing "no decision" (0-2)
- **0:** No decision-enablement assets. Relies on persuasion or discounting. Next steps are "follow up next week."
- **1:** Some decision guidance exists (e.g., options comparison) and a pilot/risk-reversal framing, but the Mutual Action Plan is incomplete or missing buyer commitments and dates.
- **2:** Complete decision guide + bounded risk reversal + MAP with owners, dates, and explicit buyer commitments. "Do nothing" cost is quantified. The buyer has a low-risk, clear path to try/decide.

## 6) Activation plan — white-glove close-to-implementation (0-2)
- **0:** Sale ends at verbal "yes" or signature. No activation definition, no onboarding steps, no follow-up cadence.
- **1:** Onboarding checklist exists and a follow-up cadence is partially defined, but activation criteria are vague and there is no scheduled kickoff or owner assignment.
- **2:** Activation criteria are explicit, kickoff is scheduled during the close, owners are assigned, 30-day success milestones are defined, and a follow-up cadence ensures "yes" does not become silence.

## 7) Learning loop + completeness (0-2)
- **0:** No iteration plan. Missing Risks/Open questions/Next steps. No tracking or review cadence.
- **1:** Some tracking and next steps exist, but the learning loop is ad hoc. Risks/Open questions are present but thin. No clear cadence for message iteration.
- **2:** Clear weekly review loop with what changes based on call feedback, tracked hypotheses, message iteration plan, and complete Risks/Open questions/Next steps. Assumptions are explicitly labeled.
