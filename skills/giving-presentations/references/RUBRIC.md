# Rubric (Score 0–2)

Score each dimension 0/1/2:
- 0 = missing / unusable
- 1 = present but weak
- 2 = clear, executable, and likely to land well

Suggested passing bar: **>= 10/14**.

## 1) Objective + ask clarity (0–2)
- **0:** Ask is missing or changes mid-way; a listener cannot state what they are being asked to do.
- **1:** Ask exists but is not crisp, or it is buried late in the talk rather than stated early and reinforced at the close.
- **2:** Ask is explicit, memorable, and easy to act on. A listener could repeat it verbatim after the talk.

## 2) Audience fit (0–2)
- **0:** Content feels generic; audience roles, incentives, and concerns are ignored.
- **1:** Some tailoring exists (e.g., audience is named), but key stakeholders’ objections or priorities are not addressed in the narrative.
- **2:** Content is tailored to what the specific audience cares about; stakeholder concerns are anticipated and addressed with proof or tradeoffs.

## 3) Narrative + contrast strength (0–2)
- **0:** No tension, no “why now,” no contrast between current state and proposed future.
- **1:** Contrast exists but is vague (“things could be better”) or unsupported by evidence.
- **2:** “What is vs what could be” is concrete, evidenced, and emotionally compelling. The audience feels the gap.

## 4) Slide clarity + structure (0–2)
- **0:** Slides are dense or confusing; multiple competing takeaways per slide; no time budget.
- **1:** Mostly clear, but some slides compete for attention, or the timing is tight with no buffer.
- **2:** Clean, single-takeaway slides with a smooth flow that fits within the time limit with buffer. A teammate could predict the talk from the outline.

## 5) Proof and credibility (0–2)
- **0:** Critical claims are unsupported or hand-wavy; no data, examples, or labeled assumptions.
- **1:** Some evidence exists but at least one key claim lacks support or relies on anecdote alone.
- **2:** Every critical claim has credible evidence (data, customer quote, benchmark) or is clearly labeled as an assumption.

## 6) De-risking (pre-brief + alignment) (0–2)
- **0:** High-stakes talk with no pre-briefing or risk plan; key decision-makers will see the ask for the first time live.
- **1:** Some pre-briefing planned but not targeted to the most important stakeholders or blockers.
- **2:** Key stakeholders are pre-briefed; surprises removed; changes from pre-briefs are tracked in a change log.

## 7) Delivery readiness (0–2)
- **0:** No rehearsal plan; high risk of rambling, reading slides, or running over time.
- **1:** One rehearsal completed or planned; timing is close but not confirmed by a timed run.
- **2:** Multiple timed runs planned; record/review step included; delivery cues (pauses, look-up-to-think, transitions) are scripted.

## Interpreting scores
- **12–14:** Solid. Proceed, but tighten weak areas.
- **9–11:** Risky. Rework the lowest two dimensions.
- **< 9:** Do not present yet. Rebuild narrative/structure and rehearse.
