# Rubric (Managing Imposter Syndrome) — Score 0/1/2

0 = missing or unusable | 1 = present but weak | 2 = clear, specific, and reusable

Suggested passing bar: **>= 7/10**

## 1) Specificity (0–2)
- **0:** Generic pep talk with no episodes captured; no concrete behaviors or triggers identified; advice is one-size-fits-all.
- **1:** Some episodes and actions are documented, but triggers are vague (“meetings make me anxious”), behaviors are not linked to costs, and the plan remains broad.
- **2:** Clear trigger-thought-feeling-behavior chains for 1-3 episodes; specific inner scripts are named; behaviors and their costs are documented; the plan directly addresses the identified patterns.

## 2) Calibration (0–2)
- **0:** Assumes all self-doubt is distortion; ignores real skill gaps entirely; no confidence-vs-competence diagnostic.
- **1:** Mentions the competence vs confidence distinction but does not operationalize it — e.g., labels are missing per trigger, or a real gap is acknowledged without a concrete learning plan.
- **2:** Each trigger is labeled as confidence gap (distortion) or competence gap (real skill deficit) with supporting evidence; competence gaps have a paired learning plan; confidence gaps have targeted reframes with counter-evidence.

## 3) Actionability (0–2)
- **0:** Advice-only output; no experiments, no exposure ladder, no schedule. The user has no concrete next action.
- **1:** Experiments exist but are not small enough to be safe-to-fail, not scheduled to specific days, or missing pass/fail signals and debrief prompts.
- **2:** 3-5 small experiments on an exposure ladder (easy to hard), each scheduled to a specific week/day, with pass/fail signals, a debrief prompt, and a plan for what happens if the experiment “fails.”

## 4) Support + sustainability (0–2)
- **0:** No support loop; plan encourages solo grinding; no maintenance or relapse protocol.
- **1:** Mentions support (e.g., “talk to your manager”) but provides no scripts, no cadence for check-ins, and no relapse plan for when imposter feelings return.
- **2:** Clear scripts for manager/mentor/peer support conversations; scheduled check-in cadence; daily/weekly maintenance routine; explicit relapse protocol (“when it comes back, do X”) with specific triggers and responses.

## 5) Safety + boundaries (0–2)
- **0:** Oversteps into therapy territory (offers diagnoses, claims to treat anxiety/depression); ignores crisis signals; requests sensitive personal health information.
- **1:** Includes disclaimers (“this is not therapy”) but boundaries are not integrated into the workflow — e.g., the safety screen is a footnote rather than a workflow gate.
- **2:** Safety boundary check is step 1 of the workflow; crisis signals trigger an explicit stop-and-refer; boundaries are stated clearly and early; no sensitive health information is requested; the pack explicitly positions itself as complementary to professional support.

## Interpreting the score
- **9–10:** Ready to execute immediately.
- **7–8:** Solid; tighten the weakest area.
- **< 7:** Rework specificity, calibration, or safety boundaries before shipping.
