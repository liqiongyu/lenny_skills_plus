# Rubric (score 0/1/2)

0 = missing / unusable
1 = present but incomplete
2 = clear, executable, reusable

Score the PMF Measurement Pack across dimensions. Use scores to decide whether to ship/share as-is or iterate.

Suggested passing bar: **>= 10/14**.

## 1) Decision usefulness (0–2)
- 0: No decision identified; the pack is purely descriptive (e.g., "here is our retention data") with no stated action that depends on the PMF finding
- 1: A decision is named (e.g., "whether to scale paid acquisition") but the pack does not clearly connect PMF outcomes to specific different actions (what you do if PMF is confirmed vs. not confirmed)
- 2: Clear decision + explicit "what we'll do differently" for each plausible outcome (e.g., "if VD% >= 40% in mid-market, approve $200K acquisition budget; if <30%, pause and run 3 activation experiments")

## 2) Segment clarity (0–2)
- 0: "All users" treated as one group; no segmentation attempted; conclusions are company-wide yes/no
- 1: Segments are mentioned (e.g., "enterprise vs SMB") but definitions are vague (no firmographic or behavioral criteria), or PMF conclusions are not broken down per segment
- 2: Crisp segment definitions with explicit criteria (e.g., "mid-market = 50-500 employees, using product >= 4 weeks, activated"); PMF status, evidence, and actions are stated per segment

## 3) Evidence quality / triangulation (0–2)
- 0: Single signal used (e.g., only Sean Ellis survey or only retention); no counts or sample sizes; no limitations acknowledged
- 1: Two or more signal types present (survey + behavior, or survey + references) but gaps/limitations are not addressed, or signals contradict each other without resolution
- 2: All three signal types present (survey + behavioral + reference-customer); each signal includes sample size/counts; limitations and confidence level are explicit; conflicting signals are reconciled

## 4) Survey rigor — Sean Ellis (0–2)
- 0: No survey conducted, or ad hoc questions used instead of the standard "very disappointed" framing; no sample definition; no "primary benefit" follow-up
- 1: Standard Sean Ellis questions used, but one or more of: unclear target population, no segment breakdown, benefit themes not synthesized, or major bias risks (survivorship, recency) unacknowledged
- 2: Standard questions with explicit sampling criteria (who was included/excluded and why); results reported with counts (n) and percentages overall + by segment; top benefit themes synthesized; bias risks listed with mitigations

## 5) Behavioral rigor — retention/engagement (0–2)
- 0: No retention or engagement data; or only vanity metrics (total sign-ups, page views) with no cohort or usage-frequency analysis
- 1: Retention/engagement data present but with unclear cohort definitions (when does the clock start?), no cadence-aware interpretation (comparing daily product retention to monthly product benchmarks), or confounders not addressed (contractual lock-in, seasonal effects)
- 2: Correct cohort definitions with explicit start event; retention curve shape described and interpreted (improving/flat/decaying); engagement frequency compared to the product's natural use cadence; confounders and instrumentation gaps identified with impact on confidence

## 6) Actionability (0–2)
- 0: No action plan, or only vague recommendations (e.g., "improve onboarding," "talk to more users")
- 1: Actions listed but missing one or more of: prioritization, hypotheses, metrics to track, owners, or timeboxes; actions are not tied to specific PMF gaps identified in the diagnosis
- 2: Prioritized action plan where each action ties to a diagnosed PMF gap, includes a hypothesis, specifies leading/lagging indicators, and has a timebox; includes a PMF re-measurement cadence with drift triggers

## 7) Completeness + hygiene (0–2)
- 0: Missing two or more required sections (context, measurement model, survey, behavioral, references, scorecard, action plan, risks); assumptions not stated
- 1: All sections present but assumptions only partially noted; Risks/Open questions/Next steps section is generic or thin (fewer than 3 items total)
- 2: Complete pack with all 8 deliverable sections; assumptions explicit and labeled with confidence; Risks/Open questions/Next steps includes at least 3 specific items with owners or next actions

### Interpretation (suggested)
- **12–14:** Strong; share broadly; execute action plan.
- **8–11:** Good directional; tighten weak areas before high-stakes decisions.
- **<8:** Rework inputs/definitions; evidence too thin or unclear.
