# Rubric (score 1–5; 3 = acceptable)

## 1) Role clarity + success definition
**0 (missing):** No success definition or milestones of any kind; plan is purely logistical.
**1** No concrete success definition; 30/60/90 section is absent or contains only generic advice (“learn the product”).
**2** Some milestones exist but are activity-based (“attend 10 meetings”) rather than outcome-based; no 1y/2y trajectory.
**3** 30/60/90 defined with basic deliverables and checks; 1y/2y may be mentioned but is vague.
**4** 30/60/90 milestones are outcome-based and realistic; 1y/2y trajectory exists but lacks measurable indicators for one or two phases.
**5** 30/60/90 + 1y/2y are crisp, measurable, and stakeholder-aligned. Each phase has clear “what done looks like” definitions.

## 2) Belonging + social integration
**0 (missing):** No social integration plan; onboarding is purely task/access-oriented.
**1** No buddy/pair plan; onboarding is mostly solo reading; day 1 has no social touchpoints.
**2** Buddy is mentioned but lacks expectations, frequency, or accountability; introductions are unstructured.
**3** Buddy + first pair exist; intros and rituals are scheduled; but coverage of key interfaces is incomplete.
**4** Social integration plan covers buddy, introductions, and key interfaces; minor gap (e.g., no explicit isolation-risk mitigation for remote hires).
**5** Social integration is intentional, timeboxed, and covers key interfaces; isolation risks are mitigated with specific mechanisms (e.g., daily check-ins week 1, virtual coffee roulette for remote).

## 3) Listening tour design + synthesis
**0 (missing):** No listening tour or stakeholder engagement plan.
**1** No structured listening tour; meetings are ad hoc; new hire has no guidance on who to meet or what to ask.
**2** A list of people to meet exists but lacks a question guide, synthesis template, or scheduling guidance.
**3** Stakeholder map + question guide + synthesis table exist; but coverage may miss critical interfaces or questions are too generic.
**4** Tour covers all critical interfaces with specific questions that force examples and trade-offs; synthesis template exists but lacks a clear mechanism to turn findings into action.
**5** Coverage is complete; synthesis yields clear themes, trade-offs, and a credible focus proposal. The 30-day state-of-the-union memo template is ready to fill.

## 4) Working agreement (relationship design)
**0 (missing):** No mention of relationship design or working agreement.
**1** Expectations and norms are implicit; manager-report relationship has no documented structure.
**2** Some expectations are stated but are one-directional (manager to report only); communication preferences and escalation paths are unclear.
**3** Basic agreement on communication, expectations, and escalation; but decision rights and feedback cadence are not fully specified.
**4** Communication, escalation, and feedback cadence are documented; decision rights are mostly clear but one or two areas remain ambiguous.
**5** Decision rights, feedback cadence, and operating rhythms are explicit and documented. Both parties' preferences and boundaries are captured.

## 5) Practicality + operational readiness
**0 (missing):** No actionable plan; only generic advice.
**1** Outputs are generic and not usable; no dates, owners, or dependencies specified.
**2** Plan has some structure (day/week breakdown) but is not calendar-ready; missing key dependencies (e.g., access provisioning, equipment).
**3** Plan is realistic and can be executed with moderate effort; most dependencies are identified but some lack owners or timelines.
**4** Plan is nearly calendar-ready with owners and timelines; one or two minor dependencies lack resolution.
**5** Plan is calendar-ready; dependencies, owners, and constraints are handled. Preboarding checklist ensures day 1 starts without friction.

## 6) Risk awareness
**0 (missing):** No risks or open questions section.
**1** No risks or open questions identified; plan assumes everything will go smoothly.
**2** Risks are identified but are generic (“hire might not ramp fast enough”) without specific mitigations or checkpoints.
**3** Risks and open questions exist with some mitigations; but mitigations lack owners or timelines.
**4** Risks are role-specific with actionable mitigations and checkpoints; one or two “smallest next actions” are missing.
**5** Risks are prioritized with specific mitigations, checkpoints, and “smallest next actions.” Open questions have owners and deadlines.

## Scoring interpretation
- **24–30:** Excellent; ready to run.
- **18–23:** Good; minor gaps to address.
- **12–17:** Risky; revise before use.
- **<12:** Not usable; rebuild the pack.
