# Rubric — Organizational Transformation Pack

Score each dimension 0–2. Total max = 14.

## 1) Outcome clarity (0–2)
- **0:** Goals are vague or framed as framework/process adoption ("implement SAFe"); no measurable outcomes defined.
- **1:** Goals are stated as outcomes but not measurable or behavior-linked; non-goals are missing.
- **2:** Goals are outcome + behavior based with clear metrics, non-goals, and a timeframe; sponsors can verify success without subjective judgment.

## 2) Diagnostic specificity (0–2)
- **0:** No system map of how work flows today; diagnosis is opinion-based with no concrete examples of bottlenecks.
- **1:** Some bottlenecks listed with limited mechanisms or evidence; missing "how the current model reinforces the problem."
- **2:** System map of idea-to-shipped flow + concrete bottlenecks with mechanisms, examples, and evidence of where feature-team model is reinforced.

## 3) Context-fit (framework hygiene) (0–2)
- **0:** Copies a popular model (Spotify, SAFe) wholesale without adaptation; no "what to borrow / what to skip" analysis.
- **1:** Tailored in parts but still framework-led; guardrails on borrowing are incomplete or generic.
- **2:** Tailored thesis with explicit guardrails on what to borrow, what to avoid, and why; rationale tied to org context, talent maturity, and product shape.

## 4) Target operating model concreteness (0–2)
- **0:** Aspirational statements only ("empowered teams"); no specific roles, cadences, or decision rights.
- **1:** Some roles and cadences defined; decision rights or required artifacts are unclear; a leader cannot answer "who decides what" on Day 1.
- **2:** Team types, roles, decision rights, cadences, and required artifacts are explicit and observable; a leader can answer "who decides what" and "what good looks like" on Day 1.

## 5) Pilot quality (0–2)
- **0:** No pilots, or pilots are big-bang (whole org simultaneously); no learning mechanism.
- **1:** Pilots exist but lack explicit hypotheses, measurable success metrics, enablement plan, or learning loop.
- **2:** 2-4 safe-to-try pilots with explicit hypotheses, measurable metrics, coaching/enablement plan, and a learning loop to adapt before scaling.

## 6) Change management + reinforcement (0–2)
- **0:** Announcement-only; no reinforcement mechanisms; resistance is not addressed.
- **1:** Comms plan exists but reinforcement mechanisms are limited; resistance map is missing or objection handling is generic.
- **2:** Stakeholder-specific comms + rituals/metrics/leader behaviors to reinforce change; resistance map informs pilot design and messaging.

## 7) Governance + measurement (0–2)
- **0:** No governance cadence, leading indicators, or escalation path; no way to know if transformation is working.
- **1:** Some metrics and cadence defined, but escalation path or rollback guardrails are unclear.
- **2:** Clear review cadence, leading indicators (not just lagging), escalation path, rollback triggers, and "framework hygiene" guardrails.

## Interpretation
- **0–6:** Not ready. Re-do intake + clarify outcomes and system diagnosis.
- **7–10:** Usable draft. Tighten decision rights, pilots, and governance.
- **11–14:** Strong. Ready to execute with iterative learning.
