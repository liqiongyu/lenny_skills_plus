# Rubric (Planning Under Uncertainty)

Score each item 0/1/2:
- 0 = missing / unusable
- 1 = present but incomplete
- 2 = clear, executable, reusable

Suggested passing bar: **>= 14/18**.

## 1) Decision clarity (0–2)
- **0:** Objective is missing or generic (“make it better”); no time horizon or decision owner named.
- **1:** Objective exists but “why now” is vague; decision type (stop/pivot/scale/commit) is unclear.
- **2:** Objective, “why now”, time horizon, decision owner(s), and decision type are all explicit.

## 2) Mode fit (0–2)
- **0:** No mode declared; plan treats crisis and exploration identically.
- **1:** Mode is stated but the plan doesn't match (e.g., peacetime approach to a crisis situation).
- **2:** Wartime vs peacetime mode is correctly chosen, justified, and the plan structure matches urgency.

## 3) Uncertainty mapping (0–2)
- **0:** No uncertainty map; unknowns are not listed or are hand-waved (“there's some risk”).
- **1:** Unknowns listed but not prioritized by impact/confidence; validation methods missing for top items.
- **2:** Unknowns/assumptions are concrete, prioritized by impact/confidence, and validation methods exist for top items.

## 4) Hypothesis quality (0–2)
- **0:** No hypotheses; or “hypotheses” are actually goals (“users will love it”) with no falsification path.
- **1:** Hypotheses exist but are not clearly falsifiable; or they don't map to a specific decision.
- **2:** Hypotheses are falsifiable, tied to a mechanism, and each maps to a concrete decision (stop/pivot/scale).

## 5) Experiment portfolio (0–2)
- **0:** No experiments defined; or experiments are “launch and see” with no defined learning goal.
- **1:** Experiments exist but are too slow, too large, or missing owner/timeline/expected learning.
- **2:** Experiments are feasible, appropriately sized; each has an owner, timeline, and expected learning.

## 6) Metrics as compass (0–2)
- **0:** No signals defined; or metrics are lagging/vanity with no directional value.
- **1:** Some metrics exist but treated as GPS (false precision); no “disprove” signals or guardrails.
- **2:** Primary signals are leading and directional; guardrails exist; pack avoids false precision and includes “disprove” signals.

## 7) Buffers + contingencies (0–2)
- **0:** No buffers; plan assumes everything works on the first attempt.
- **1:** Buffers exist but are blanket (“add 20% to everything”) rather than tied to specific risks; contingency triggers are vague.
- **2:** Buffers are explicit and proportional to named uncertainties; contingency paths have operational triggers (“if X then Y”).

## 8) Cadence + comms (0–2)
- **0:** No learning/decision cadence; stakeholders get updates only when asked.
- **1:** Cadence exists but agenda is vague; decision logging or stakeholder update format is missing.
- **2:** Learning/decision cadence exists with a runnable agenda; stakeholder updates and decision logging are covered.

## 9) Actionability (0–2)
- **0:** Risks/open questions/next steps are missing or listed without owners or time bounds.
- **1:** Risks and next steps exist but some lack owners or are too vague to execute without follow-up meetings.
- **2:** Risks/open questions/next steps are actionable with owners and time bounds; a team could execute without additional interpretation.
