# Rubric — Post-mortems & Retrospectives (0–2 per dimension)

Score the Post-mortems & Retrospectives Pack before finalizing. Total max = 12.

## 1) Clarity of scope and safety
- **0:** Goal is unclear; blame framing is present (timeline or factors point at individuals); roles (facilitator, scribe, decision owner) are missing.
- **1:** Goal and roles exist; mostly blameless but some language or structure implicitly singles out individuals; constraints or audience are ambiguous.
- **2:** Goal and audience are crisp; explicit blameless norms established; facilitator, scribe, and decision owner are assigned; constraints and anonymization rules are clear.

## 2) Evidence and timeline quality
- **0:** Hand-wavy narrative; no timestamps, links, or sources; facts and hypotheses are indistinguishable.
- **1:** Partial timeline with some timestamps; some evidence provided but facts and hypotheses are mixed together without labels.
- **2:** Clear timeline with timestamps and links/evidence; facts vs hypotheses are explicitly separated and labeled; impact is quantified.

## 3) Systems thinking (contributing factors)
- **0:** Individual blame or single-point cause ("Person X broke it"); no systemic analysis.
- **1:** Some system factors identified but clustering is shallow (single "why"); "why it made sense" lens is missing; changeable conditions are not distinguished from one-offs.
- **2:** Multi-factor systems view across People/Process/Product/Tech/Comms/Environment; "why it made sense" is captured for top factors; changeable system conditions are identified with at least 3 levels of "why."

## 4) Decisions and actionability
- **0:** Vague recommendations ("we should be better at X"); no owners, dates, or success signals.
- **1:** Some actions have owners but success signals are weak or follow-up cadence is missing; "later ideas" are not explicitly parked.
- **2:** Concrete decisions with action tracker (owner, due date, success signal, follow-up date); top actions address top contributing factors; "later ideas" are explicitly parked.

## 5) Learning dissemination and institutionalization
- **0:** No shareout; learning stays within the retro participants; no recurring review mechanism.
- **1:** Shareout exists but lacks a recurring cadence or designated owner; learning is documented but not socialized.
- **2:** Clear 1-page TL;DR shareout + recurring "Impact & Learnings" review with owner, inputs, and a defined audience.

## 6) Kill criteria / triggers (if applicable)
- **0:** Triggers are absent or meaningless ("discuss it later"); no pre-committed actions.
- **1:** Some triggers defined but thresholds are vague or actions are unspecific; ownership is unclear.
- **2:** Observable signals with specific thresholds and pre-committed actions (pause, pivot, kill, escalate); ownership is clear for each trigger.

## Interpreting scores
- **10–12:** Ship as-is.
- **7–9:** Ship with explicit assumptions + a short "unknowns" list.
- **< 7:** Do another intake round (max 5 questions) before finalizing.
