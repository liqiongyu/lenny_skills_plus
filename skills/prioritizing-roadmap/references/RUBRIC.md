# Rubric (Score the Pack)

Score each category **0–2** and sum. Use the notes to decide what to fix.

Scale:
- **0 = Missing / unacceptable**
- **1 = Partial / unclear**
- **2 = Strong / clear / decision-ready**

## Scoring table

| Category | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Decision framing clarity | No decision stated; unclear what will be decided or by whom. | Decision is mentioned but scope, audience, or deadline is vague (e.g., “prioritize stuff for next quarter”). | Decision, audience, deadline, and success criteria are explicit; a stakeholder knows exactly what is being decided and when. |
| Season framing usefulness (why now + bets + non-goals) | No season context; opportunities are listed in a vacuum without macro context. | Season is mentioned but bets are generic or non-goals are missing; “why now” is unclear. | Clear season name, 3-5 specific bets, explicit non-goals, and a compelling “why now” that stakeholders can restate. |
| Opportunity inventory quality (problem/outcome, evidence, conviction) | Raw feature requests or ideas with no outcome framing, no evidence, no conviction labels. | Items are framed as outcomes but evidence is sparse and conviction levels are inconsistent or missing for some items. | Every item has a problem/outcome statement, evidence note, and conviction label (Known/Belief/Hypothesis); discovery vs delivery is split. |
| Common-currency scoring reproducibility | No scoring model; ranking is implicit or purely intuitive. | A scoring model exists but scales are ambiguous enough that two people would produce very different rankings. | Scales are defined with concrete anchors; two people using the model independently would produce similar relative tiers. |
| Tradeoffs explicitness (“no’s”, parking lot, constraints) | No parking lot; no explicit “no’s”; constraints are not applied to the ranking. | Some tradeoffs mentioned but parking lot is missing or constraints are listed without showing how they affected the ranking. | Explicit parking lot with rationale per item; constraints visibly shaped the ranking; “no’s” are named with reasons. |
| Roadmap coherence (sequencing + cadence) | No sequencing; items are listed but not ordered by dependencies, learning, or capacity. | Sequencing exists (Now/Next/Later or quarterly) but dependencies or update cadence are missing. | Sequenced roadmap with dependencies mapped, capacity accounted for, and a rolling update cadence defined. |
| Balance (core vs growth vs quality vs big bets) | Roadmap is entirely one type (e.g., all new features, no quality/debt investment). | Some balance visible but one category (e.g., big bets or quality) is absent without explicit rationale. | Portfolio is intentionally balanced across core, growth, quality, and big bets; imbalance is acknowledged and justified. |
| Alignment readiness (narrative + comms + owners) | No decision narrative; roadmap is a spreadsheet with no explanation of “why these, why now.” | Decision narrative exists but is generic; comms plan or owners are missing. | Clear “why these, why now” narrative; comms plan names channels, audiences, and cadence; each top item has a named owner. |
| Risks/open questions/next steps quality | Section missing or boilerplate (“more research needed”). | Risks and questions are listed but generic; next steps lack owners or deadlines. | 3+ specific risks with mitigation ideas, open questions tied to named owners, and next steps with clear actions and timelines. |

## Interpretation
- **16–18:** Strong. Ready to share and align.
- **12–15:** Usable but has gaps; fix the lowest-scoring categories.
- **<12:** Not decision-ready; revisit decision framing, season framing, and the scoring model.
