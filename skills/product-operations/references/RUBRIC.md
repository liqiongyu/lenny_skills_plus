# Rubric (Product Operations)

Score each dimension **0/1/2**.

- **0** = missing or unusable
- **1** = present but incomplete
- **2** = clear, executable, decision-ready

Suggested passing bar: **>= 7/10** with no dimension at 0.

## 1) Clarity of charter + boundaries (0–2)
- **0:** No charter; or a vague “we help PMs” statement with no boundaries or ownership defined.
- **1:** Charter has scope and non-goals but engagement model is unclear; or PM decision rights are not explicitly preserved.
- **2:** Crisp charter with scope, non-goals, engagement model, preserved PM decision rights, and measurable success criteria.

## 2) Practical operating model (0–2)
- **0:** No RACI or ownership model; interfaces between teams are undefined.
- **1:** RACI exists but some interfaces lack owners or escalation paths; intake/prioritization is unclear.
- **2:** RACI is complete; interfaces reduce friction; prioritization and intake are workable with named owners.

## 3) Cadence + artifacts produce decisions (0–2)
- **0:** Meetings without defined outputs; no templates; rituals are “just meetings.”
- **1:** Rituals produce outputs and some templates exist, but the set is too large to sustain or missing key artifacts.
- **2:** Minimal ritual set with high leverage; templates exist for all core artifacts; each ritual produces a concrete output.

## 4) Insights pipeline drives action (0–2)
- **0:** Feedback is collected but has no routing, ownership, or decision path.
- **1:** Intake/triage/routing defined and owners assigned, but reporting cadence or signal quality improvement plan missing.
- **2:** Clear “decision path” from signal to action; reporting cadence defined; mechanism to improve signal quality over time.

## 5) Implementation + adoption (0–2)
- **0:** No rollout plan; or plan attempts to launch everything to all teams simultaneously.
- **1:** 30/60/90 plan exists with a pilot and metrics, but iteration loop or change comms are missing.
- **2:** Adoption plan is credible with a pilot; iteration loop, change comms, and success measurement are explicit.
