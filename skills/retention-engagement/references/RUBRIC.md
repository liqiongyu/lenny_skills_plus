# RUBRIC (Retention & Engagement)

Score each item 0/1/2:
- 0 = missing / unusable
- 1 = present but incomplete
- 2 = clear, executable, reusable

Suggested passing bar: **>= 16/20**.

## 1) Problem fit (0–2)
- **0** — Scope is wrong (addresses acquisition or PMF instead of retention/engagement) or no boundaries stated.
- **1** — Scope is roughly correct but boundaries with neighboring skills (user-onboarding, designing-growth-loops) are vague or missing.
- **2** — Retention/engagement/activation scope is correct; explicit NOT-for boundaries distinguish this from onboarding, growth loops, and PMF work.

## 2) Input contract (0–2)
- **0** — No inputs gathered; proceeds on generic assumptions without acknowledging gaps.
- **1** — Some inputs collected but missing-info strategy is absent; key items (segments, baseline metrics, constraints) are partially addressed.
- **2** — Minimal required inputs are listed; missing-info strategy follows “ask up to 5, then assume”; assumptions are labeled with confidence.

## 3) Metric clarity (0–2)
- **0** — Metrics are undefined or use ambiguous terms (“improve retention”) without specifying behavior, segment, or time window.
- **1** — Metrics are named (e.g., D7 retention) but lack segment specificity or guardrail definitions.
- **2** — Retention/engagement/activation metrics are explicitly defined with behavior, segment, time window, and at least one guardrail metric.

## 4) Diagnosis quality (0–2)
- **0** — No structured diagnosis; jumps directly to solutions without identifying failure modes.
- **1** — Some analysis present but does not distinguish between activation failure, engagement decay, and monetization churn; or lacks segment breakdown.
- **2** — Primary failure mode + biggest leak are identified with evidence (or labeled assumptions); at least 2 segments analyzed; diagnosis drives lever selection.

## 5) Activation/aha definition (0–2)
- **0** — No activation definition, or definition is opinion-based (“users understand the value”).
- **1** — Activation is defined behaviorally but lacks a threshold, time window, or validation plan.
- **2** — Behavioral activation definition with specific threshold (e.g., “completes X within Y days”), tied to retention correlation, and includes a validation plan.

## 6) Insight-to-action mapping (0–2)
- **0** — Insights are listed but not converted into testable hypotheses; no connection between diagnosis and proposed actions.
- **1** — Hypotheses exist but are not consistently tied to specific failure modes or lack measurable leading indicators.
- **2** — Every hypothesis ties to (a) a diagnosed failure mode and (b) a measurable leading indicator; hypotheses are converted into rules + checks.

## 7) Experiment design (0–2)
- **0** — No experiment cards; recommendations are vague (“try push notifications”).
- **1** — Experiment cards exist but lack one or more of: hypothesis, success metric, guardrail, or rollback plan.
- **2** — Experiment cards have hypothesis, success metrics, guardrails, instrumentation requirements, and rollout/rollback plan.

## 8) Prioritization (0–2)
- **0** — No prioritization; all ideas presented as equal or in arbitrary order.
- **1** — Some ranking exists but scoring method is opaque or top 3 are not feasible within stated constraints.
- **2** — Backlog is prioritized with a transparent method (e.g., Impact x Confidence / Effort); top 3 are feasible within constraints and have clear rationale.

## 9) Execution plan (0–2)
- **0** — No sequencing; experiments are listed without timeline or dependencies.
- **1** — 30/60/90 plan exists but lacks dependency awareness or measurement checkpoints.
- **2** — 30/60/90 plan is sequenced, dependency-aware, includes measurement checkpoints, and the next 2 weeks of work are unblocked.

## 10) Safety + trust (0–2)
- **0** — Recommends dark patterns (e.g., hidden cancellation, guilt-based notifications) or ignores privacy; no guardrails for “mounting loss.”
- **1** — Mentions ethical considerations but lacks specific guardrails for switching costs, notification frequency, or data usage.
- **2** — Avoids dark patterns; respects privacy; includes explicit user-control guardrails for “mounting loss” and notification frequency; flags regulatory considerations.
