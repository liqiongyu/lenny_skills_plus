# RUBRIC (Definition of Done)

Score each dimension 0/1/2:
- 0 = missing / unclear
- 1 = present but weak
- 2 = clear, executable, and likely to work

Suggested passing bar: **≥ 16/20**.

## 1) Objective clarity (0–2)
- **0:** No stated objective; the meeting invite could be about anything.
- **1:** Objective exists but is vague (“discuss project status”) and does not state what changes after the meeting.
- **2:** Objective is specific and outcome-oriented; you can answer “what will be different after this meeting?” in one sentence.

## 2) Right meeting format (0–2)
- **0:** Format is not chosen or is clearly wrong (e.g., decision meeting with no decision owner, or status meeting labeled “strategic”).
- **1:** Format is named but does not fully match the goal (e.g., operational sync used for a topic that needs deep strategic discussion).
- **2:** Format matches the goal (operational / strategic / decision / workshop); status-in-disguise is avoided.

## 3) Discover/Discuss/Decide separation (0–2)
- **0:** No separation; context, discussion, and decision are all expected to happen live with no preparation.
- **1:** Some separation exists (e.g., a pre-read is mentioned) but discovery still bleeds heavily into live time, or decisions are not explicitly recorded.
- **2:** Pre-read covers discovery; at least 60% of live time is discussion; decisions are explicitly captured as they happen.

## 4) Pre-read quality (0–2)
- **0:** No pre-read exists, or it is a raw data dump with no framing.
- **1:** Pre-read includes context but is missing options, open questions, or a clear “what to come prepared to discuss.”
- **2:** Pre-read includes context, options, and open questions; a participant can arrive ready to discuss without a live info dump.

## 5) Agenda quality (0–2)
- **0:** No agenda, or agenda is a single line (“discuss project”).
- **1:** Agenda lists topics but lacks time boxes, explicit outputs per segment, or has too many topics (>3) for the available time.
- **2:** Timed agenda with 1-3 topics; each segment has an explicit expected output; discussion time is preserved.

## 6) Facilitation readiness (0–2)
- **0:** No facilitator assigned; no script or prompts; the meeting will self-organize (or not).
- **1:** Facilitator is named but there are no prompts, “if stuck” branches, or ground rules.
- **2:** Roles (facilitator, decision owner, note-taker, timekeeper) are assigned; script includes priming, prompts, and “if stuck” branches.

## 7) Decision/action capture (0–2)
- **0:** No decision log or action items template; decisions will be implicit or forgotten.
- **1:** Decision log exists but is missing owners or due dates, or only some decisions are captured.
- **2:** Every decision is recorded with rationale; every action item has an owner and due date.

## 8) Close-out alignment (0–2)
- **0:** Meeting ends with no summary; participants leave with different understandings of what happened.
- **1:** Some closing summary exists but misses one of: what we decided / who does what / who else needs to know.
- **2:** Meeting ends with explicit answers to all three: what we decided, who does what by when, and who else needs to know.

## 9) Follow-up quality (0–2)
- **0:** No follow-up sent; decisions and actions stay in the facilitator's head.
- **1:** Follow-up is sent but late (>24h), incomplete, or does not update relevant trackers.
- **2:** Follow-up message sent within 24h with decisions, owners, deadlines, and tracker updates.

## 10) Hygiene improvements (0–2)
- **0:** No reflection on whether the meeting should exist, be shorter, or be restructured.
- **1:** A general suggestion is made (e.g., “consider shortening”) but no specific actionable change is proposed.
- **2:** At least one specific, actionable change to reduce future meeting waste (split strategic from operational, cancel a recurring meeting, shorten from 60 to 30 min, move status to async).
