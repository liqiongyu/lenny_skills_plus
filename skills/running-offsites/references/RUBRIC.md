# Running Offsites — Scoring Rubric

Score each item 0/1/2:
- 0 = missing / unusable
- 1 = present but incomplete
- 2 = clear, runnable, and likely to produce good outcomes

Suggested passing bar: **>= 16/20**.

## 1) Scope + boundaries (0–2)
- **0:** No scope defined; the offsite could be about anything; conflict/HR boundaries are not mentioned.
- **1:** Scope is stated but vague (e.g., “strategy offsite”) with no clear when-not-to-use or conflict escalation path.
- **2:** Clear when to use and when not to use; conflict/HR boundaries are explicit; neighboring skills are referenced for redirects.

## 2) Input contract (0–2)
- **0:** No minimum inputs defined; the offsite is designed without knowing goals, participants, or constraints.
- **1:** Some inputs are gathered but key items are missing (e.g., desired outcomes, budget, or known tensions) and assumptions are not labeled.
- **2:** Minimum inputs are explicit; missing info is handled with labeled assumptions and 3-5 clarifying questions.

## 3) Outputs are artifact-first (0–2)
- **0:** Deliverables are vague (“have a good discussion”) with no concrete artifacts.
- **1:** Some deliverables are named but not all have clear “done” definitions or output order is undefined.
- **2:** Every deliverable is concrete and checkable (decisions, plans, principles); output order is defined; each session produces a named artifact.

## 4) Agenda quality (0–2)
- **0:** No timed agenda, or agenda is all talks with no interactive sessions.
- **1:** Agenda exists with some timing but is talk-heavy (<40% interactive) or sessions lack explicit output artifacts.
- **2:** Timed agenda with at least 60% interactive/co-creation time; every block has a named output artifact; breaks and connection time are included.

## 5) “Defrag the day” (0–2)
- **0:** No distraction controls; laptops open, Slack pinging, no breaks.
- **1:** Some controls mentioned (e.g., “laptops down suggested”) but not enforced through norms or facilitation script.
- **2:** Distraction controls are explicit and built into the facilitation script (laptops down, scribe pattern, breaks, physical tools, “out of routine” expectations).

## 6) Prework protects time (0–2)
- **0:** No prework; participants arrive cold and spend offsite time on context-setting.
- **1:** Prework exists but is too light (e.g., “read this doc”) or too heavy (participants won't do it); no prompts or assignments.
- **2:** Prework pack includes pre-read, prompts, and assignments that gather inputs asynchronously; participants arrive ready to decide, not just get informed.

## 7) Facilitation runnable (0–2)
- **0:** No facilitation script; the offsite depends entirely on the organizer improvising.
- **1:** Facilitation notes exist but are missing key elements (opening framing, norms, decision capture method, or disagreement handling).
- **2:** Run-of-show includes opening framing, norms, “laptops down” rule, decision capture method, breakout instructions, and how to handle disagreement safely. A facilitator could run it with minimal additional context.

## 8) Logistics frictionless (0–2)
- **0:** No logistics plan; venue, food, travel, and tech are not addressed.
- **1:** Logistics are partially covered but missing key items (accessibility, contingency plans, or AV setup).
- **2:** Complete logistics checklist covering venue, rooms, supplies, food, travel, tech, accessibility, and contingency plans. For bursts: repeatable booking/logistics process.

## 9) Memory + follow-through (0–2)
- **0:** No post-offsite plan; decisions and actions stay on whiteboards or in people's heads.
- **1:** Some follow-up exists but is missing owners, dates, comms to non-attendees, or review checkpoints.
- **2:** Post-offsite pack includes decisions with owners/dates, action plan, follow-up comms to stakeholders not present, and a review checkpoint date.

## 10) Safety + realism (0–2)
- **0:** Forced vulnerability exercises or conflict is ignored entirely; no HR/legal escalation path.
- **1:** Some awareness of safety but elephants are either avoided or addressed without appropriate care.
- **2:** Avoids forced vulnerability; names elephants at a safe level; escalates genuine conflict to HR/leadership; realistic about what an offsite can and cannot fix.
