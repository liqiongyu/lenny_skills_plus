# Sales Qualification — Scoring Rubric (0–2)

Score each category from **0 (absent)** to **2 (strong)**. A "ship-ready" pack scores **≥ 10/14** with no dimension at **0**.

## 1) ICP clarity + segmentation
**0 — Absent:** ICP is vague or missing; no exclusions defined; buyer and user are not distinguished.
**1 — Partial:** ICP is defined with some segmentation (e.g., primary segment identified) and a few exclusions, but attributes are not fully observable or triggers are missing.
**2 — Strong:** 1-3 clear segments with observable firmographic/behavioral attributes, buying triggers, and explicit exclusions. A rep can classify an inbound lead in under 2 minutes.

## 2) Disqualification strength (time protection)
**0 — Absent:** No hard disqualifiers; reps have no mechanism to say "no" quickly. Every lead gets a full discovery call regardless of fit.
**1 — Partial:** Some disqualifiers exist but rely on subjective judgment (e.g., "not a good fit") or are incomplete (cover only 1-2 of the key dimensions). No disqualification talk track.
**2 — Strong:** Fast, objective disqualifiers that can be applied in the first 10-15 minutes (e.g., wrong segment, below revenue threshold, no budget authority). Includes a graceful disqualify talk track and a nurture handoff.

## 3) Scorecard quality + consistency
**0 — Absent:** No scorecard, or vague questions without weights, anchors, or thresholds. Two reps would produce wildly different outcomes.
**1 — Partial:** Scorecard has reasonable criteria (5-8) with some weights, but anchors are vague (e.g., "strong budget" without defining what that means). Thresholds for accept/nurture/reject are soft.
**2 — Strong:** 5-8 weighted criteria with concrete scoring anchors (e.g., "2 = budget confirmed by VP-level sponsor; 1 = budget likely but unconfirmed; 0 = no budget discussion"). Must-pass criteria defined. Two reps score the same deal within one tier.

## 4) Script effectiveness (diagnose then decide)
**0 — Absent:** No script, or the script is a pitch deck disguised as discovery. Does not reach a qualification decision.
**1 — Partial:** Mostly diagnostic questions covering pain and need, but gaps in urgency, decision process, or stakeholder mapping. The call may not reliably produce a go/no-go decision.
**2 — Strong:** A 20-30 minute call flow that surfaces current state, pain/impact, trigger/urgency, stakeholders, and constraints. Ends with a dated next step (accept), a nurture handoff, or a graceful close-out. Questions map to scorecard criteria.

## 5) CRM usability + pipeline hygiene
**0 — Absent:** No structured notes template; stages are undefined or have no exit criteria; next steps are not tracked.
**1 — Partial:** A notes template exists and some stage exit criteria are defined, but hygiene rules are incomplete (e.g., no stalled-deal policy, no maximum stage age).
**2 — Strong:** Structured notes template with required fields mapping to scorecard criteria. Stage exit criteria with observable evidence for each transition. "No next step, no stage" rule enforced. Stalled-deal policy with maximum stage age and escalation.

## 6) Rollout + measurement plan
**0 — Absent:** No adoption plan, no metrics, no iteration loop. The pack is delivered without guidance on how to implement it.
**1 — Partial:** Some rollout steps (e.g., "train the team") and KPIs mentioned, but the plan is not operational (no timeline, no calibration sessions, no feedback loop).
**2 — Strong:** A 2-week rollout plan with specific calibration sessions (score 5 real deals together). KPIs tied to funnel outcomes (qualification-to-close rate, time-in-stage, disqualification rate). Iteration loop defined (revisit after 20-30 calls).

## 7) Completeness + actionability
**0 — Absent:** Multiple key artifacts missing (no scorecard, no script, or no stage criteria). Output is generic guidance rather than usable tools.
**1 — Partial:** Most artifacts are present but some have ambiguity or gaps. Risks/open questions section is generic or missing.
**2 — Strong:** All artifacts are copy/paste ready. Risks are specific and mitigated. Open questions have owners. Next steps are executable this week. The team can start using the pack immediately.

## Decision rule
- **Ship** if total score is **≥ 10/14** and no dimension scores **0**.
- **Revise** if any dimension scores **0** or total is below **10**.
