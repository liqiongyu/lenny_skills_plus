# Rubric (Scoping & Cutting Pack)

Score each category 0/1/2:
- 0 = missing / unclear
- 1 = present but incomplete
- 2 = clear, executable, decision-ready

Suggested passing bar: **>= 12/16**.

## 1) Decision + appetite clarity (0–2)
- **0:** Decision (ship/defer/stop) is missing or ambiguous; no DRI named; no time budget stated.
- **1:** Decision and DRI exist but appetite is vague (“a few weeks”) or constraints are not listed.
- **2:** Decision, DRI, appetite (specific timebox), and constraints are all explicit and agreed.

## 2) Outcome + hypothesis quality (0–2)
- **0:** No outcome statement; or outcome is internal (“build feature X”) rather than user-centric.
- **1:** Outcome is user-centric but hypothesis is missing or untestable (“users will like it”).
- **2:** Outcome is specific and user-centric; hypotheses are falsifiable with defined success/failure criteria.

## 3) Metrics + guardrails (0–2)
- **0:** No success metrics defined; or metrics are vanity-only (e.g., “page views”).
- **1:** Success metric exists but is ambiguous or easily gamed; guardrails are missing or generic.
- **2:** Success metrics are measurable and unambiguous; guardrails protect trust/quality/cost with explicit thresholds.

## 4) Minimum Lovable Slice coherence (0–2)
- **0:** No slice defined; or the “slice” is a partial subsystem that no user can complete end-to-end.
- **1:** Slice covers a flow but is missing non-goals, or includes items that aren’t essential to the core value.
- **2:** Slice is end-to-end, delivers core value, must-haves are minimal, and non-goals are explicit.

## 5) Cut list rigor (0–2)
- **0:** No cut list; scope is implicitly “everything”.
- **1:** Cut list exists but lacks rationale; or deferred items have no revisit triggers.
- **2:** Every cut/deferral has explicit rationale tied to outcome and risk; “revisit when” triggers are defined.

## 6) Validation plan strength (0–2)
- **0:** No validation plan; the team plans to build everything before testing value.
- **1:** Validation plan exists but targets low-risk assumptions or requires building the full system to test.
- **2:** Targets the top unknowns; runs without building the full system; success criteria are defined before running.

## 7) Delivery plan realism (0–2)
- **0:** No delivery plan; or milestones are just a list of tasks with no dates or owners.
- **1:** Milestones exist but don’t clearly fit the appetite; dependencies or owners are missing.
- **2:** Milestones fit the appetite, show a clear path to “done”, and dependencies/owners are explicit.

## 8) Scope-change guardrails (0–2)
- **0:** No change-control mechanism; scope can grow without any trade-off.
- **1:** “Trade, don’t add” is mentioned but the escalation path or decision owner is unclear.
- **2:** “Trade, don’t add” policy is clear and enforceable; escalation path and decision owner are defined.
