# Rubric (Score 0/1/2)

Score each item 0/1/2:
- 0 = missing / unusable
- 1 = present but incomplete
- 2 = clear, executable, reusable

Suggested passing bar: **>= 16/20**.

## 1) Discovery & triggering (0–2)
- **0:** Description does not match real user phrasing; skill would not be found for “set OKRs” or “quarterly goals” requests.
- **1:** Description partially matches but misses key synonyms or category context.
- **2:** Description + scope match real user phrasing (“set OKRs”, “quarterly goals”, “key results”, “goal setting”) and include category.

## 2) Boundaries (0–2)
- **0:** No “When NOT to use” section or boundaries are missing/generic.
- **1:** Some boundaries listed but missing explicit redirects to neighboring skills (e.g., North Star, vision, roadmap).
- **2:** “When NOT to use” is explicit with skill redirects, including when to do strategy/North Star work first.

## 3) Input contract (0–2)
- **0:** No minimum inputs listed; no missing-info strategy.
- **1:** Inputs listed but missing-info strategy is vague or missing.
- **2:** Minimum inputs are listed and missing-info strategy is clear (ask up to 5, then assume with labeled assumptions).

## 4) Output contract (0–2)
- **0:** Deliverables are unnamed or unordered.
- **1:** Deliverables listed but unclear ordering or missing checkability criteria.
- **2:** Deliverables are explicit, ordered, and checkable with clear format expectations.

## 5) Alignment quality (0–2)
- **0:** No alignment to company goal; objectives float without strategic anchor.
- **1:** Alignment mentioned but linkage is indirect (more than one step away) or undocumented.
- **2:** Each objective is one step away from the company goal and the linkage is documented with rationale.

## 6) Objective quality (0–2)
- **0:** Objectives are activities (“launch X”, “build Y”) not outcomes; or more than 5 objectives listed.
- **1:** Objectives are outcome-oriented but too many (4+) or do not clearly change weekly prioritization.
- **2:** Objectives are outcome-oriented, few in number (1-3), and change weekly prioritization with clear “why now.”

## 7) KR quality (0–2)
- **0:** KRs lack definitions, baselines, or targets; they are vanity metrics or activity counts.
- **1:** KRs have some definitions and targets but baselines are missing, owners are unassigned, or metrics are not independently verifiable.
- **2:** KRs are measurable with definitions/baselines/targets/owners/data sources; two analysts would compute the same number.

## 8) Anti-gaming + guardrails (0–2)
- **0:** No guardrails; ratio risks and gaming vectors are not addressed.
- **1:** Some guardrails mentioned but not specific per-KR; missing denominator/quality checks for ratio metrics.
- **2:** Ratio risks are handled with denominator KRs; per-KR failure modes and guardrails exist with detection mechanisms.

## 9) Systems + cadence (0–2)
- **0:** No systems/habits section; no review cadence defined.
- **1:** Review cadence exists but no “default-on” systems; or systems listed without owners/cadence.
- **2:** “Default-on” systems/habits are specified per objective with owners; review/grading cadence creates a learning loop (weekly + mid-cycle + end-of-cycle).

## 10) Shareability (0–2)
- **0:** Pack requires extensive verbal explanation to be understood; missing context or next steps.
- **1:** Pack is mostly self-contained but assumptions are unlabeled or next steps are vague.
- **2:** Pack can be shared with stakeholders as-is; assumptions are labeled and next steps are explicit with owners and timelines.
