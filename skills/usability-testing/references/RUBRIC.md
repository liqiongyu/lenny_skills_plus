# Rubric (Usability Testing)

Score each dimension **1–5** (1 = poor, 3 = acceptable, 5 = excellent). Include notes and a short improvement plan for any score ≤ 3.

## 1) Decision clarity
- **1:** No decision; “test for feedback” with no stated outcome or timeline.
- **2:** A vague goal exists (“see if users like it”) but no specific decision or success bar.
- **3:** Decision exists but is broad; success bar is fuzzy; “what we’ll do if…” scenarios are missing.
- **4:** Decision + deadline stated; success criteria exist but thresholds are soft.
- **5:** Clear decision + deadline + explicit “what we’ll do if…” scenarios (ship / fix / retest).

## 2) Stimulus/prototype strategy
- **1:** Stimulus is unclear or mismatched to the question; no rationale for the chosen fidelity.
- **2:** A stimulus is identified but it’s over-built or under-built for the question being asked.
- **3:** Reasonable choice; limitations aren’t documented; what’s simulated vs real is unclear.
- **4:** Good stimulus choice with rationale; simulated vs real is mostly documented; minor gaps.
- **5:** Cheapest valid stimulus chosen with explicit rationale; what’s simulated vs real is explicit; limitations are documented and mitigated.

## 3) Task design quality
- **1:** Tasks are leading, unrealistic, or refer to specific UI labels/locations (e.g., “Click the gear icon”).
- **2:** Some tasks are scenario-based but others leak UI labels or test the happy path only; no error/recovery tasks.
- **3:** Mostly realistic scenario-based tasks; a few tasks still bias outcomes; starting states are inconsistent.
- **4:** Neutral tasks with starting states and success criteria; at least one error/recovery task; minor wording improvements possible.
- **5:** Neutral, scenario-based tasks with starting states and success criteria; includes error/recovery tasks and micro-friction probes; no UI label leakage.

## 4) Participant fit + coverage
- **1:** Participants are “anyone available”; poor fit for the scenario; no inclusion/exclusion criteria.
- **2:** Some criteria exist but are demographic-only; no behavioral/situational filters; no edge cases.
- **3:** Participant profile mostly fits with behavioral criteria; some gaps acknowledged; edge cases not planned.
- **4:** Clear inclusion/exclusion criteria; core + some edge cases covered; recruiting plan is realistic.
- **5:** Clear inclusion/exclusion criteria with deliberate coverage (core + edge cases); realistic recruiting plan with backups; “who NOT to test” is explicit.

## 5) Operations readiness
- **1:** Logistics, recording, and roles are missing; session would likely fail or produce unusable data.
- **2:** Basic logistics exist but moderator guide is incomplete; note-taking roles unassigned; no backup plan.
- **3:** Basics covered (guide, logistics, recording); some operational risks remain (no backups, unclear tooling).
- **4:** Moderator guide is complete; roles assigned; tooling confirmed; minor gaps in backup planning.
- **5:** Moderator guide, tooling, backups, and note-taking roles are clear and test-ready; pilot session planned or completed.

## 6) Evidence capture quality
- **1:** Findings are opinionated summaries; little evidence recorded; no verbatims or timestamps.
- **2:** Some notes exist but are inconsistent across sessions; quotes are paraphrased; no issue log structure.
- **3:** Evidence exists per session but is inconsistent or hard to trace to specific tasks; issue log is partial.
- **4:** Clean notes with verbatims per session; issues are linked to tasks/screens; minor traceability gaps.
- **5:** Notes include verbatims + timestamps/screens per session; every issue is backed by concrete examples (quote/screenshot/step); observation vs interpretation is clearly separated.

## 7) Synthesis + actionability
- **1:** A flat list of observations with no severity, frequency, or prioritization; no fix recommendations.
- **2:** Issues are listed with some grouping but severity/frequency is missing; recommendations are generic.
- **3:** Issues are prioritized; recommendations exist but lack expected impact or owner suggestions; no quick-win separation.
- **4:** Prioritized issues with severity + frequency; recommendations include directional impact; quick wins identified.
- **5:** Prioritized issues with severity/frequency + directional impact; clear quick wins separated from larger fixes; follow-up plan (retest, experiment, design iteration) is defined.

## 8) Ethics + privacy
- **1:** Privacy/consent ignored entirely; PII or recordings handled without policy; risky data handling.
- **2:** Consent is mentioned but not scripted; recording policy unclear; no data retention plan.
- **3:** Basic consent/minimization included; recording policy stated; storage/retention unknowns are noted.
- **4:** Consent + recording explicit; data minimization practiced; retention policy defined; minor gaps.
- **5:** Minimization + consent + storage/retention considerations are explicit; high-risk cases (minors, medical, etc.) are gated on approval; deletion timeline specified.

## Suggested overall rating
- **Excellent:** avg ≥ 4.2, no dimension < 4
- **Good:** avg 3.5–4.1, no dimension < 3
- **Needs work:** avg < 3.5 or any dimension < 3
