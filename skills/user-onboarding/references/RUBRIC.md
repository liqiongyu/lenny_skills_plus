# RUBRIC (User Onboarding Skill Output)

Score each item 0/1/2:
- 0 = missing / unusable
- 1 = present but incomplete
- 2 = clear, executable, reusable

Suggested passing bar: **>= 16/20**.

## 1) Goal clarity (0–2)
- **0** — No measurable goal stated; vague aspiration like “improve onboarding” with no baseline, target, or date.
- **1** — Goal names a metric (e.g., activation rate) but is missing baseline, target, or date; or guardrails are absent.
- **2** — Goal includes baseline, target, and date (e.g., “Improve 7-day activation from 9% to 18% by end of Q3”); guardrails are explicit.

## 2) Segmentation (0–2)
- **0** — No segments defined; treats all users identically.
- **1** — Segments are mentioned but the onboarding plan is not tailored to them; or only one generic segment is used when the product has clearly distinct user types.
- **2** — 1-2 segments are defined with distinct jobs-to-be-done; the plan is tailored to each segment's path to value.

## 3) Journey map + friction log (0–2)
- **0** — No journey map; jumps to solutions without mapping the current flow.
- **1** — Journey steps listed but lack measurable checkpoints or friction ranking; frictions are noted but not tied to specific fixes.
- **2** — FTUE steps are mapped end-to-end with measurable checkpoints; top frictions are ranked by drop-off impact and tied to proposed fixes.

## 4) Activation definition (0–2)
- **0** — No activation definition, or it is opinion-based (“user sees the value”), not behavioral.
- **1** — Activation is behavioral but missing a time window, threshold, or validation plan; or not correlated with retention.
- **2** — Activation/aha moment is behavioral, measurable, includes a threshold and time window, tied to retention correlation, with a validation plan.

## 5) First 30 seconds quality (0–2)
- **0** — No “first 30 seconds” design; or the design is a passive carousel/tour with no interactive element.
- **1** — A first-session concept exists but the “win” is vague, takes longer than 30 seconds without justification, or relies on passive content.
- **2** — The “first win” is concrete, achievable within ~30 seconds (or proxy is justified with data), avoids passive tours, and focuses on interactive value delivery.

## 6) First mile plan quality (0–2)
- **0** — No milestone plan; onboarding is described as a single monolithic flow.
- **1** — Milestones exist but are vague (“set up your profile”), lack metrics, or don't demonstrably reduce time-to-value.
- **2** — 3-6 milestones are specific and realistic; each includes mechanics (progressive disclosure, templates, defaults) and a leading-indicator metric; milestones reduce time-to-value.

## 7) Measurement plan (0–2)
- **0** — No measurement plan; metrics are mentioned in passing but no event schema or data sources defined.
- **1** — Key metrics are named but event schema is incomplete; dashboards or data sources are unspecified.
- **2** — Minimum viable event schema is defined with specific events/properties; dashboards and owners (if known) are specified; instrumentation gaps are flagged.

## 8) Experiments (0–2)
- **0** — No experiment backlog; recommendations are vague (“optimize the flow”).
- **1** — Experiments listed but lack prioritization scoring, or top experiments are missing “win/lose/learn” criteria or rollout/rollback plans.
- **2** — Backlog is prioritized with a clear scoring model (e.g., ICE); top experiments have hypothesis, success metric, guardrail, and rollout/rollback plans.

## 9) Safety + ethics (0–2)
- **0** — Uses dark patterns (forced permissions, guilt-based copy, hidden skip options) or requests PII/secrets.
- **1** — No overt dark patterns but ethical considerations are not explicitly addressed; privacy handling is vague.
- **2** — Avoids dark patterns; respects privacy; no secrets/PII requested; explicitly addresses consent, progressive permission requests, and user control.

## 10) Finalization (0–2)
- **0** — No risks, open questions, or next steps; output is unstructured or not actionable.
- **1** — Risks/open questions/next steps are present but superficial or generic; output structure has gaps.
- **2** — Includes specific **Risks**, **Open questions**, and **Next steps**; output is structured, copy/paste-ready, and high signal.
