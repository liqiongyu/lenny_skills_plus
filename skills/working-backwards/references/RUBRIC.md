# Rubric (0/1/2 scoring)

Score each item 0/1/2:
- 0 = missing / unusable
- 1 = present but incomplete
- 2 = clear, concrete, decision-ready

Suggested passing bar: **>= 14/18**.

## 1) Customer clarity (0–2)
- **0**: No customer problem stated; PR describes features or internal goals only.
- **1**: Customer segment named; problem is described in company language, not customer language.
- **2**: Problem today is specific and customer-readable; a target user would recognize the pain.

## 2) “Why now” urgency (0–2)
- **0**: No urgency or timing rationale; could be done anytime.
- **1**: Trigger mentioned but opportunity cost of waiting is unstated.
- **2**: Trigger and timing are credible; opportunity cost is explicit.

## 3) Options quality (0–2)
- **0**: Only one PR option; no alternatives explored.
- **1**: 2-3 options exist but are variations of the same approach (e.g., scope tiers), not meaningfully different solution shapes.
- **2**: 2–3 PR options are meaningfully different and trade-offs are surfaced.

## 4) Selected PR quality (0–2)
- **0**: No clear winner selected; or selected PR is vague marketing copy.
- **1**: Selected PR has a headline and summary but “how it works” or “who it's not for” is missing.
- **2**: Headline/summary, how-it-works, and boundaries are concrete.

## 5) FAQ completeness (0–2)
- **0**: No FAQ section; or only 1-2 softball questions.
- **1**: Customer FAQs present but internal objections (cost, feasibility, legal) are weak or missing.
- **2**: Customer + internal (business/ops/tech/legal) FAQs cover top objections; hard questions answered honestly.

## 6) Backcasting plan realism (0–2)
- **0**: No plan; or plan is a list of phases with no dates, owners, or dependencies.
- **1**: Milestones exist but some owners are “TBD” and critical dependencies are unaddressed.
- **2**: Milestones, owners, dependencies, and “done” criteria exist and are plausible.

## 7) Metrics + guardrails (0–2)
- **0**: No metrics; or metrics are vanity (e.g., “page views”) with no connection to the PR's promise.
- **1**: Metrics exist but are not computable or not owned; guardrails missing.
- **2**: Success metrics and guardrails are computable, owned, and connected to the narrative.

## 8) Risk handling (0–2)
- **0**: No risk analysis; or “risks: none.”
- **1**: Risks listed generically; pre-mortem not done or mitigations are “monitor.”
- **2**: Pre-mortem produces specific failure modes with mitigations/monitors.

## 9) Decision readiness (0–2)
- **0**: Pack requires a meeting to explain; cannot be read async.
- **1**: Readable async but key open questions are unlisted or the go/no-go criteria are unclear.
- **2**: The pack enables an async “go / no-go / revise” decision; open questions are explicit.
