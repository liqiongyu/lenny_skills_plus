# Rubric (Score the Pack)

Score each category **0–2** and sum. Use the notes to decide what to fix.

Scale:
- **0 = Missing / unacceptable**
- **1 = Partial / unclear**
- **2 = Strong / clear / decision-ready**

## Scoring table

| Category | 0 (Missing / unacceptable) | 1 (Partial / unclear) | 2 (Strong / clear / decision-ready) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Customer value (POV) | Metric measures internal activity (e.g., deploys, support tickets closed), not customer-delivered value | Metric relates to customer value but framing is company-centric or the "value moment" is vague | Metric directly measures customer-perceived value delivery; value moment is specific and observable |
| Clarity & simplicity | Metric requires a paragraph to explain; stakeholders cannot restate it | Metric is understandable but name or definition has ambiguity (e.g., "engagement") | Metric can be explained in one sentence; any stakeholder can use it as a decision tie-breaker |
| Measurability (unambiguous spec) | No formula, window, or inclusion rules defined; two analysts would compute different numbers | Formula exists but window, segmentation, or edge-case rules are incomplete | Formula, unit, window, inclusion/exclusion rules, segmentation, and example calculation are all specified |
| Controllability (within horizon) | No team can move the metric within the planning horizon; purely lagging | Metric is partially controllable but input levers are vague or unproven | At least 2-3 concrete levers exist that teams can execute within the horizon to move the metric |
| Leading inputs/proxies defined | No driver tree or input metrics identified | Driver tree exists but some drivers lack measurement or actionable levers | 3-7 drivers with metrics, owners, and at least one realistic lever per driver |
| Guardrails & ecosystem impact | No guardrails; gaming risks or ecosystem harm not considered | Guardrails mentioned but not specific (e.g., "maintain quality") or missing for key gaming vectors | Specific guardrails with thresholds for each gaming vector; ecosystem impact assessed |
| Cross-functional usefulness (tie-breaker) | Metric is relevant to one team only; cannot resolve cross-team prioritization disputes | Metric is understood across teams but not clearly usable as a tie-breaker for resource allocation | Multiple teams can use the metric to independently evaluate whether their work matters |
| Operationalization (owners/cadence) | No owner, no dashboard, no review cadence defined | Owner exists but dashboard, review cadence, or decision rules are incomplete | Owner, dashboard, review cadence, and decision rules (what to do when metric moves) are all defined |
| Risks/open questions/next steps quality | No risks or next steps section | Risks listed but generic; next steps lack owners or timelines | Risks are specific with mitigations; next steps have owners, timelines, and clear validation criteria |

## Interpretation
- **16–18:** Strong. Ready to share and operationalize.
- **12–15:** Usable but has gaps; fix the lowest-scoring categories.
- **<12:** Not decision-ready; revisit narrative, definition clarity, and controllable inputs.
