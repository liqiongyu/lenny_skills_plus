# Rubric (Score 0–2)

Score each dimension 0/1/2:
- 0 = missing / unusable
- 1 = present but weak
- 2 = clear, executable, and ready to send

Suggested passing bar: **>= 8/12**. If any dimension is 0, revise before sending.

## 1) Outcome + ask clarity (0–2)
- **0:** No stated outcome or ask; a reader cannot tell why this was sent or what they should do.
- **1:** Ask exists but is vague, buried in the middle, or missing a deadline/response path.
- **2:** Outcome is crisp and stated in the first few lines; the decision/action path is effortless for the reader; deadline is explicit.

## 2) “How” specificity (actionability) (0–2)
- **0:** Mostly context and rationale; no concrete next steps, owners, or dates.
- **1:** Next steps exist but are missing owners, dates, or dependencies; a reader would need to ask “so what do I do?”
- **2:** Highly actionable plan with concrete steps, named owners, dates, and dependencies. A reader could execute without a follow-up meeting.

## 3) Structure + scanability (clarity at scale) (0–2)
- **0:** Wall of text; no TL;DR, no headings, no bullets; the lede is buried or absent.
- **1:** TL;DR and headings exist, but the information hierarchy is weak (e.g., key message is in paragraph 3, or sections are out of scan order).
- **2:** Optimized for both skim and deep read: TL;DR up front, headings in scan order (Ask > Context > Details > Next steps), bullets, short paragraphs. A skim-reader captures the point in under 60 seconds.

## 4) Correctness + completeness (given inputs) (0–2)
- **0:** Missing key facts; likely to confuse or mislead the reader; contradictions present.
- **1:** Includes necessary context and constraints, but gaps exist (missing links, unstated assumptions, or anticipated reader questions not addressed).
- **2:** Accurate, complete, and anticipates key reader questions. Assumptions are labeled. Links and references are included.

## 5) Tone + trust (0–2)
- **0:** Defensive, blaming, passive-aggressive, or wildly mismatched to the audience (e.g., casual tone to the board).
- **1:** Direct and respectful but flat; does not acknowledge uncertainty or invite constructive response.
- **2:** Builds trust; acknowledges uncertainty honestly; appropriate confidence level for the audience; invites constructive response where relevant.

## 6) Source of truth + follow-through (0–2)
- **0:** No canonical location for the latest version; no follow-through plan; readers will inevitably ask “where is the latest?”
- **1:** Canonical doc or link exists, but next update cadence, owners, or decision trail are missing.
- **2:** Canonical doc/link is established with clear owners, next update cadence, and decision trail. Reduces future meetings by making decisions, owners, and cadence crystal clear.
